Part 1 · Lesson 1

Bug, feedback or question?

Three kinds of "this isn't right"

Not everything that goes wrong is a bug, and each kind has its own door. Sending it to the right one gets it to the right person faster.

Bug

A tool did something it shouldn't: a crash, a wrong number, a button that does nothing, a page that looks broken. Use Report a bug in that tool, or at the bottom of any page on the site.

Playtest feedback

The rules or tools worked as written, but something about play wasn't right: unclear, slow, unbalanced or not fun. Use the playtest form your channel links to.

Question

You're not sure how something works. Ask in the 20 Below Discord; someone will answer, and if it turns out to be a bug, they'll point you to the button.

Where does it go?

The Battle Tracker froze when I added a sixth creature.

Everyone sprinted every round because it was cheap.

Can a Gift and a Boon stack?

The Character Creator showed 12 Ki, but my Attributes should give 14.

Nobody at the table could agree on what "cover" means.

The Save button on the Owlbear sheet does nothing.

Initiative took ten minutes with eight players.

Where do I find the Difficulty chart?

Part 1 · Lesson 2

A report that gets fixed

The team reads every report, but they can only fix what they can see. A good report lets someone who wasn't there make the same thing happen.

The Report a bug form, field by field

Press Report a bug in the tool where it happened. The tool fills in the technical details for you, so you only write what you saw.

  1. Short titleSay what went wrong in a few words, specific enough to tell it apart from other bugs: "Initiative order doesn't update", not "Tracker broken".
  2. SectionPick the step or tab where it happened. It sends your ticket to the part of the tool the team will look at first.
  3. What happenedWhat you were doing, and what went wrong. Include the numbers you saw.
  4. What you expected (optional)What should have happened. This is often how the team tells a bug from a misunderstanding.
  5. Steps to reproduce (optional)1, 2, 3 from a fresh start. If the team can make it happen, they can fix it.
  6. Contact, then SendAdd a Discord name or email if you'd like a reply, then press Send report. The form shows your ticket number.

Part 1 · Lesson 3

After you press Send

Your report becomes a numbered ticket, like #12, in the team's bug forum on Discord. The form shows you the number; keep it if you want to ask about it later.

What the tags mean

New

It's arrived and hasn't been looked at yet. Every ticket gets a first look within 48 hours.

Confirmed

The team made it happen too. It's on the list to fix.

Fixed

The fix has shipped. The thread says which version.

Won't fix

The team decided to leave it as it is, and the thread says why. Sometimes that's because the tool is working as the rules say.

Duplicate

Someone reported the same thing first. The thread links to that ticket, where the conversation continues.

Nothing from your character is ever sent.

Open "Sent with your report" in the form to see exactly what goes with it: the app, its version, the page, your browser, your screen size and the time.

If you'd like a reply

If you left a contact, the team may reply in the ticket's thread or reach out to ask for a detail.

If the form can't reach the team, it offers Copy report instead, so nothing you wrote is lost. Paste it into the 20 Below Discord.

Part 2 · Lesson 4

Telling us what happened at the table

Playtest feedback isn't about bugs. It's about how the game plays: the moments that confused, dragged or didn't feel right. Use the link pinned in the playtester channel.

Pick the kind

Unclear rule

The table couldn't agree on what a rule means. Quote the rule if you can.

Too slow

Something slowed the table down. Say roughly how long it took.

Balance

Too strong, too weak, or a number felt off. Say what happened in play, not just the number.

Not fun

It worked as written but didn't feel good. Tell us the moment.

Tool issue

How play works inside a tool, like a step that's awkward mid-session. Crashes still go to Report a bug.

Session report

Notes on a whole session: what went well, what dragged or confused, and anything else.

What a good report looks like

Balance · Sprint costs too little Ki.

Movement. Everyone sprinted every round from round 2 on, so nobody ever walked. Maybe Sprint should cost 2 Ki? Played Oct 3, four players, I was GM.

After you send

Your ticket

It becomes P# and a number, in the private playtest forum, tagged New plus its kind.

Discussing

The team is talking it over; watch the thread, and they may ask you a question.

Changed

A rule or tool was updated because of it.

Won't change

The team considered it and kept things as they are, and the thread says why.

Part 3 · Lesson 5

The triage loop

Every ticket gets a tag and a first reply within 48 hours. Feral runs triage on both forums; other team members step in when a ticket is handed to them.

The loop, every day or two

Open #bug-reports and #playtest-feedback and filter by New.

  1. Read itRead the whole ticket, including "Details from the app": the app, its version and the page.
  2. Reproduce it (bugs)Use the same app and version, and follow the steps. For playtest tickets, find the rule or tool it names.
  3. Tag itBugs: Confirmed, Won't fix or Duplicate. Playtest: Discussing, or Won't change. Leave it New only while you wait for an answer.
  4. Reply in the threadEvery ticket gets a human reply: "Confirmed, thanks", "Can you tell us which browser?", or the reason it won't change.
  5. Hand it offIf it needs someone else, mention them in the thread and say what you need.

A reporter should never wonder whether anyone saw their ticket.

Part 3 · Lesson 6

Making the call

Most tickets are easy. These are the ones to slow down for.

Can't reproduce

Reply asking for what's missing (steps, version, browser) and leave it New. If there's no answer in two weeks, tag Won't fix with "Couldn't reproduce; reply here if it happens again."

Working as the rules say

If the tool matches the rules, it's not a bug. Tag Won't fix, quote the rule, and if the rule itself seems wrong, suggest the player send it as playtest feedback.

Already reported

Tag it Duplicate and link the first ticket in the thread ("Duplicate of #N"), so the conversation stays in one place and duplicates are counted separately.

Part 3 · Lesson 7

Closing the loop

A ticket isn't done when the fix is merged. It's done when the reporter knows.

From Confirmed to Fixed

  1. Fix and releaseThe fix ships in a tagged release, for example Battle Tracker 0.3.8.
  2. Tag FixedChange the ticket's tag from Confirmed to Fixed.
  3. Say which versionReply in the thread: "Fixed in Battle Tracker 0.3.8. Thanks for reporting it!"
  4. Playtest ticketsWhen a rule or tool changes because of feedback, tag Changed and say what changed and where.

Thank the reporter every time. People who see their reports fixed report again.
