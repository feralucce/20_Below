Open by Design

ORC License

20 Below is released under the ORC License on purpose: we want everyone to be able to create content for 20 Below, not just play it. Write your own adventures. Design your own setting. Publish a supplement and sell it - without asking permission, and without paying a fee for the mechanics underneath. The system is the foundation; what you build on it is yours.

That's not just a nice sentiment - the ORC License makes it a legal guarantee. The core mechanics (the roll, the Attributes, the Skill and Gift/Boon/Flaw systems) are Licensed Material, free for anyone to build on, remix, and publish commercially, forever, irrevocably. A short list of specific creative content - the 20 Below name, individual Gift/Boon/Flaw flavor text, the apps themselves - stays Reserved. Everything else is open.

What's Open vs. Reserved

Open - Licensed Material

The core mechanics: the 2d10 roll, Difficulty, Advantage/Disadvantage, the five Attributes and their sub-stats, Ki, combat resolution, and the Gift/Boon/Flaw/Skill systems themselves - their point costs, structure, and how they resolve. Build a genre book, a setting, a full commercial product on top of this, freely.

Reserved - Not Licensed

The title "20 Below" and its branding; the specific names and flavor text of individual Gifts, Boons, and Flaws already published; the 20 Below Character Creator and Desktop apps (source code, design, implementation); and any genre or setting content - like Backwater Static - not expressly released as Licensed Material.
