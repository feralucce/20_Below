Changelog

Newest entries first. Starts 2026-08-20 - not backdated to earlier development history.

Rules

  • 8-20-2026 1147 Added an Ammo Recovery rule for Bow/Crossbow and Thrown weapons: recover half the ammo spent (rounded down) if you control the battlefield after a fight, unlike firearm cartridges which aren't reusable.
  • 8-20-2026 1130 Added reload rules, a longstanding gap: a new Reload Combat Action, with cost varying by how the weapon actually loads (magazine/speed-loader/chain-fed and crossbow take one action, a tube-fed shotgun takes a Slow action, a quiver-drawn recurve bow reloads free). New Reload column on weapons.md's Basic Weapons table.
  • 8-20-2026 1020 Built out the Scars mechanic fully: Poise now goes negative (new Humiliated status, no death threshold), Mental Scars finally defined (was flagged as TBD), Battle Scars now accumulate and can be healed away after a Month by Healing/Regeneration. New Flaw: Shaken Confidence. New docs/scars.html with 27 Physical, 23 Mental, and 15 Social scar flavor options.
  • 8-20-2026 0927 Added 9 new Boons (Animal Rapport, Compact Frame, Iron Grip, Lie Detector, Never Winded, Quick Study, Restful Trance, Unshakeable Alibi, Weathered). Also newly defined Short Rest/Full Night's Rest duration and frequency, a real rules gap Restful Trance surfaced.
  • 8-20-2026 0841 Added 3 new Flaws: Item of Power (a Gift depends on an external item), Ritual Required (a Gift needs preparation time), Incantations (a Gift needs a spoken trigger).
  • 8-20-2026 0830 Added 14 new Resources (Safehouse Network, Underworld Reputation, Medical Access, Legal Counsel, Media Contact, Black Market Access, Spiritual Standing, Corporate Backing, Informant Network, Signature Possession, Sanctuary Standing, Guild Standing, Debts Owed, Territory), full 5-level tables for each.
  • 8-20-2026 0817 Resource creation cost halved: 1 point per level instead of 2 (pool stays 6 points, now buys 6 levels of Resources total instead of 3).
  • 8-20-2026 0812 Created changelog. Added 6 Gifts (Necromancy, Drone Swarm, Berserker, Conjured Armory, Memory Manipulation, Forcefield) and 1 Boon (Always Connected). Defined Milestone Award, the Combat Actions list, and Limiter buy-off costs. Various fixes and cleanup.

App

  • 8-20-2026 1039 Fixed the character sheet's Poise track to match the new negative-Poise rule (Humiliated status, correct below-0 rest recovery cap). Wired up the Limiter buy-off control on the Advancement tab. Fixed the Gift Adder "Buy" button to charge the correct Advancement XP rate instead of the creation-time cost.
  • 8-20-2026 0812 Applied site-wide brand color theming. Rebuilt the Dice Roller. Fixed Import, Load, and Export functions.
