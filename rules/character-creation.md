# Character Creation

Every character is built in the same sequence, broad concept down to concrete numbers. Follow the steps in order - later steps depend on choices made earlier (your Attributes set your sub-stat pools, your sub-stats set your Descriptors, and so on).

## Walkthrough

1. **Write a one-line concept.** A short sentence capturing who this character is and how they move through the world. Not mechanical on its own, but it'll shape your choices below, especially Nature and Flaws.
2. **Assign your Attributes.** Each of the five Attributes (Earth, Air, Fire, Water, Moira) starts at a floor of **1**. Distribute **20 points** across the five, raising any of them up to a maximum of **10** at creation. See [Attributes](core-mechanic.md#attributes).
3. **Split each Attribute into its two sub-stats.** Each Attribute generates a pool of points equal to its own rating, divided however you like between its two sub-stats - not an even split. See [Sub-Category Allocation](core-mechanic.md#sub-category-allocation).
4. **Read off your derived stats.** These aren't purchased - they're computed directly from the sub-stats you just set in step 3:
   - **Health Levels** = 5 + Health
   - **Poise** = 5 + Presence
   - **Sanity** = 5 + Psyche
   - **Defense** = 10 - Atropos
   - **Carrying Capacity** = Potence² x 10 kg
   - **Ki** = average of Soak, Initiative, Ferocity, Stamina, and Atropos, doubled
5. **Collect your Descriptors.** Free, one per point spent in step 3 - write a short word or phrase for each, capturing a distinct flavor of that sub-stat. See [Sub-Stat Descriptors](core-mechanic.md#sub-stat-descriptors).
6. **Spend your four creation pools**: Gifts (21), Boons (8), Resources (6), and Discretionary (10, spendable across the other three). See the pool breakdown below.
7. **Take Flaws (optional).** Each Flaw grants Discretionary points equal to its own point value, on top of the flat 10 from step 6. See [Flaws](flaws.md).
8. **Pick your Skills.** Every character gets the GM's [Everyman Skills](skills.md#everyman-skills) free at Trained. Beyond that, spend a **15-point Skills Pool** - see the breakdown below.
9. **Pick or write a Nature.** Choose one of the [starter Natures](fate.md#nature) or [write your own](fate.md#writing-a-custom-nature).
10. **Start with 1 Fate Token**, matching the flat daily regeneration rate.

The character is ready to play.

## Pools

| Pool | Points | Funds |
|---|---|---|
| Gifts | 21 | [Gifts](gifts.md) |
| Boons | 8 | [Boons](boons.md) |
| Resources | 6 | [Resources](resources.md) |
| Skills | 15 | [Skills](skills.md) |
| Discretionary | 10, plus more from Flaws | Any of the four pools above |

### Gifts Pool

Each Gift Level costs a flat **3 points**. A Gift bought to Level 3 costs 9 points total; to Level 5, 15 points.

An Adder (see [gifts.md](gifts.md#points)) costs either **3 points (Lesser)** or **6 points (Greater)**, paid once to unlock the option permanently. This is separate from the Ki cost of actually using the Adder, which is paid per the Gift's own text each time it's used. Every Gift should offer at least 3 Adders.

A Limiter (see [gifts.md](gifts.md#points)) is a permanent, creation-time restriction chosen for one specific Gift. Each Limiter taken drops the cost of every Level of that Gift by 1 point. A character may stack as many different Limiters on a Gift as they want, but no combination can ever drop a Level's cost below 1 point.

### Boons Pool

Spent per [boons.md](boons.md)'s own Trivial/Lesser/Greater/Legendary (1/3/5/7) point costs.

### Resources Pool

Spent per [resources.md](resources.md)'s own flat 2-points-per-level cost.

### Skills Pool

Each Skill tier costs a flat **1 point**, cumulative with every lower tier already climbed - reaching Trained costs 2 points total (Novice + Trained), reaching Adept costs 3, Expert 4, Master 5, the same shape [Gift Levels](#gifts-pool) use. The pool is **15 points** at creation - enough to reach Master in three Skills, or spread more thinly across many.

This is a creation-time budget only. Post-creation advancement stays XP-funded per [Training Tiers](skills.md#training-tiers), exact cost per tier still open - that's a progression question for later, separate from getting a character built and to the table now.

### Discretionary Pool

Every character starts with **10 Discretionary points**, flat, no strings attached. These can be spent in any of the four pools above, wherever the extra room is needed.

Taking a [Flaw](flaws.md) grants additional Discretionary points equal to the Flaw's assigned points (1-5, or the Level taken for a Leveled Flaw). The GM sets a per-campaign cap on how many Discretionary points a character can bank this way, since there's no universal number that fits every table's tolerance for min-maxing through weaknesses.

An extra [Sub-Stat Descriptor](core-mechanic.md#sub-stat-descriptors) can be bought this way too, 1 Discretionary point per Descriptor.
