# Combat

## Combat Order

1. **Initiative** — roll/determine turn order (mechanics TBD, see [Air's Initiative sub-stat](core-mechanic.md)).
2. **Declare intent**, in order from **slowest to fastest** — everyone states what they intend to do before anyone acts, slowest characters committing first.
3. **Act**, in order from **fastest to slowest**. Because intents were declared slowest-first, the fastest characters get to act on full information — an edge for speed, on top of just going first.

### Interrupted Intents

If something that happens during step 3 **eliminates another character's declared intent** (e.g. their intended target is removed, their intended action is no longer possible), that character gets a **reactive roll** — the specific Skill/Attribute pairing and Difficulty are still TBD, but it defaults to **average Difficulty**.

- **Success** — the character declares a **new intended action** and acts normally on their turn.
- **Failure** — the character declares a **new intended action** but acts at **Disadvantage** (see [Advantage/Disadvantage](core-mechanic.md#advantage--disadvantage)).

Open questions and everything else about combat (initiative mechanics, the reactive roll's exact Skill/Attribute/Difficulty, damage application, death/incapacitation) are tracked in [design-log.md](../docs/design-log.md#open-questions).
