# Premade Gifts

Source material and worked examples for [gifts.md](gifts.md), pulled from eleven power systems plus general comic book / anime / fantasy / sci-fi genre conventions. Everything below is inspiration and raw material, paraphrased rather than reproduced — none of it is a literal port of another game's rules.

## Source Cross-Reference

- **Werewolf: The Apocalypse** (Gifts, taught by spirits): heightened senses, physical enhancement (claws, speed, resilience), small-scale elemental control, fear/awe effects, camouflage, healing, shapeshifting refinements, spirit communication, pack-coordination effects.
- **Mage: The Ascension** (Spheres, nine broad reality-affecting domains each rated 1-5): matter/object transformation, life/biological manipulation, mind/thought influence, forces/energy manipulation, spirit-world interaction, time perception and manipulation, space/distance manipulation, raw magical essence, fate/probability. The closest structural match to our 5-level Gift model.
- **Changeling: The Dreaming** (Arts applied to Realms): illusion/deception, object charming, nature/wild magic, movement and travel, fate-reading, dream-crafting, true-name binding.
- **Wraith: The Oblivion** (Arcanoi, also 5-level power trees — another close structural match): incorporeal movement, possession of objects or the living, emotional/fear projection, fate perception, memory manipulation, spectral combat, reshaping one's own form, drawing power from the living world.
- **Hunter: The Reckoning** (Edges, granted by conviction): truth-sensing, supernatural perception, resilience, minor protective wards, self-healing, righteous fury, compelling honesty.
- **Exalted** (Charms, tightly-scoped trees tied to an Ability + Essence): wuxia/anime-flavored escalating special moves — perfect defenses, read-intentions social Charms, instant mastercraft creation, total-recall investigation, elemental aspects (Fire/Water/Earth/Wood/Air) for Dragon-Blooded-style characters.
- **Adventure!** (pulp heroes — Dynamism, Dark Tomorrow, Enigma origins): peak physical prowess, gadgeteering/inventing, mentalism (mind-reading, illusion), mesmerism, heroic luck/destiny effects (danger sense, catching a bullet).
- **Pendragon**: contributes less raw power material (mostly Traits/Passions and limited high-tier magic), but the core idea — an emotional Passion fueling an extraordinary feat — is worth carrying forward as a Gift *trigger* concept, plus faith-based blessings/healing.
- **Scion** (Purviews granting Boons to demigods): Epic Attributes (superhuman Strength/Dexterity/Stamina etc.), elemental/weather domains (Fire, Frost, Sky), Illusion, Health, Darkness/Death, Chaos, Guardian (wards/protection), Journeys (fast travel/teleport), Animal (command/shapeshift), Fate-adjacent Moon/Stars purviews.
- **In Nomine** (angelic/demonic Songs): super speed/flight (Motion), universal communication (Tongues), light manipulation, social charm/harmony, protective shielding, superhuman body (Numinous Corpus), shapeshifting (the Skin), destructive force (Thunder), healing/vigor, purification/revival (Salvation).
- **Hero System** (point-buy comic-book powers): the standard superhero archetype list — energy blast, super strength, flight, invulnerability, telepathy, telekinesis, stretching, shapeshifting, speedster powers, mental control, force fields, teleportation, regeneration, size change, elemental control, illusion, luck powers.
- **Heroes Unlimited** (Palladium): Mutant/Bio-Manipulation/Psionics/Robotic/Alien categories — notably strong on cybernetics/bionics and a clean Physical/Sensitive/Super split for psychic powers (self-healing & biofeedback; empathy & clairvoyance; telekinesis & telepathy & mind-bolt).

## Power Concept Catalog

A broad breadth-first list of Gift *concepts* — names and a one-line hook, not fully leveled. Treat each as a starting point: when a player actually takes one, work out its 5 levels together per [gifts.md](gifts.md#creating-new-gifts). Organized by theme, drawing on all the sources above plus general comic/anime/fantasy/sci-fi convention.

### Physical Enhancement
- **Overwhelming Might** — raw physical strength beyond normal human limits.
- **Unbreakable** — resistance to physical harm, injury, and the environment.
- **Fleet-Footed Fury** — superhuman speed and reaction time.
- **Iron Lungs** — need for air, food, sleep, or rest is drastically reduced or removed.
- **Second Wind** — rapid recovery from exhaustion, injury, or exertion.
- **Titan's Grip** — the ability to lift, carry, or throw far beyond normal capacity.

### Elemental & Energy Manipulation
- **Ember Touch** — pyrokinesis (see worked example below).
- **Frostbound** — control over cold and ice.
- **Storm's Herald** — command of wind, lightning, and weather (see worked example below).
- **Tidecaller** — control over water in its forms.
- **Stoneheart** — command over earth and stone.
- **Voltaic Pulse** — generate, direct, and absorb electrical energy.
- **Radiant Burst** — generate and project raw light/energy as force.

### Mental / Psychic
- **Mind's Whisper** — telepathy: sense or communicate thought.
- **Iron Mind** — mental resistance to intrusion, fear, and manipulation.
- **Silent Push** — telekinesis: move objects with will alone.
- **Borrowed Feeling** — empathy: sense or subtly influence others' emotions.
- **Second Sight** — clairvoyance/precognition: perceive distant or future events.
- **Ghost in the Wires** — technopathy: sense and influence machines and electronics.

### Biological / Shapeshifting & Regeneration
- **Skin of the Beast** — shapeshifting into an animal form (see worked example below).
- **Living Mend** — accelerated self-healing.
- **Adaptive Flesh** — the body reshapes to survive a hostile environment (cold, pressure, toxin).
- **Mimic's Gift** — temporarily take on a trait or capability observed in another creature.
- **Undying Vigor** — resistance to poison, disease, and fatigue.

### Social / Charisma / Emotional
- **Chorus of Concord** — voice and presence that soothes conflict and inspires trust (see worked example below).
- **Commanding Presence** — natural authority that makes others want to follow your lead.
- **Silver Tongue** — words land with uncanny persuasive force.
- **Read the Room** — an instinctive, almost supernatural grasp of others' true intentions.
- **Bound by Oath** — a promise made to you carries real, felt weight on the one who made it.

### Movement & Spatial
- **Step Between** — short-range teleportation.
- **Weightless** — flight or unassisted levitation.
- **Through the Cracks** — phasing/intangibility, passing through solid matter briefly.
- **Long Road Home** — Journeys-style fast, safe travel across long distances.
- **Wall-Runner** — freedom of movement across any surface, gravity be damned.

### Temporal
- **Stolen Moment** — briefly speed up personal perception/action relative to the world.
- **Echo of What Was** — perceive an object or place's recent past.
- **Borrowed Time** — briefly delay an effect's onset or a consequence's arrival.
- **Frozen Instant** — briefly halt or drastically slow something else in time.

### Fate, Luck & Probability
- **Fortune's Favor** — bend probability slightly in your own favor.
- **Ill Omen** — impose a run of bad luck on someone else.
- **Threadreader** — glimpse the likely consequences of a choice before it's made.
- **Twist of Fate** — force a coincidence into being at a critical moment.

### Light, Darkness & Illusion
- **Trick of the Light** — minor visual illusions and misdirection.
- **Veil of Unseeing** — become overlooked, unremarkable, or outright invisible.
- **Shadow Step** — travel through or hide within darkness.
- **Radiant Face** — project a light or aura that reveals truth, wards off darkness, or dazzles.

### Sound & Vibration
- **Voice of Thunder** — project sound or force as a weapon or tool.
- **Perfect Pitch** — hear and identify anything, including the inaudible or distant.
- **Resonant Shatter** — sound or vibration that breaks, cracks, or unravels physical structure.
- **Song Unheard** — emotional or magical effects carried through music/voice.

### Life, Death & Spirit
- **Beyond the Veil** — perceive and interact with the dead/spirit world (see worked example below).
- **Mercy's Hand** — hasten a peaceful, painless end where one is inevitable.
- **Borrowed Vitality** — draw on life force (your own or another's, willing or not) to fuel an effect.
- **Last Rites** — lay the dead properly to rest, or keep them from resting at all.

### Technology, Constructs & Bio-Tech
- **Grafted Steel** — cybernetic/bionic augmentation integrated into the body.
- **Spark of Invention** — build working gadgets and devices well beyond expected means.
- **Waking the Machine** — animate and command a construct or simple mechanism.
- **Field Repair** — restore or improve technology and machinery on the fly.

### Nature & Animal
- **Beast Speech** — communicate with and calm animals.
- **Green Thumb** — accelerate plant growth or bend it to your will.
- **Call of the Wild** — summon and briefly command nearby animals.
- **Weathersense** — read and mildly influence local weather patterns.

### Cosmic / Divine / Reality-Warping (rare, high-tier)
- **Voice of Creation** — words that briefly reshape small aspects of reality.
- **Unerring Hand** — mastercraft creation, building something perfect on the first try (see worked example below).
- **Mantle of the Divine** — a visible, tangible mark of supernatural favor or authority.
- **Worldwalker** — step briefly outside reality's normal rules to travel or observe.

## Worked Examples

Six fully defined Gifts demonstrating the 5-level format from [gifts.md](gifts.md) — each level cumulative, each a specific new capability rather than a bigger number.

### Ember Touch (inspired by Mage's Forces Sphere)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sense heat and open flame within a room, and tell whether something recently burned. |
| 2 | Cause an existing flame to flare, dim, or shift direction; ignite something already primed to catch. |
| 3 | Conjure and sustain a small controlled flame from nothing, and shape it into simple forms. |
| 4 | Hurl a burst of true fire at a target or small area; redirect an existing large fire's spread. |
| 5 | Command fire at significant scale — bend a raging blaze to your will, or extinguish a large fire instantly. |

### Skin of the Beast (inspired by Werewolf Gifts)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Minor animal traits bleed through — sharper senses, faint claws — subtle enough not to draw a second look. |
| 2 | Partial shift on command — claws, fangs, or fur emerge, aiding combat or environment without changing your silhouette. |
| 3 | Full transformation into one specific animal form, retaining your own mind. |
| 4 | The shift sharpens — faster transformation, plus an intermediate hybrid war-form. |
| 5 | Total mastery of the shift — instant transformation, and the ability to lend a lesser version of this Gift's traits to someone else briefly. |

### Beyond the Veil (inspired by Wraith's Arcanoi)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | See and hear what lingers past death — residual impressions, faint spirits, echoes of strong emotion tied to a place. |
| 2 | Communicate with what you sense — spirits can hear and respond to you, even if no one else can perceive them. |
| 3 | Reach partly into the space between — touch, or be touched by, the intangible, briefly. |
| 4 | Cross over bodily for short periods, walking unseen and unfelt by the living. |
| 5 | Move freely between the world of the living and whatever lies beyond, and guide others across with you. |

### Storm's Herald (inspired by Scion's Sky Purview)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Feel a storm coming long before anyone else, and read the wind and sky with total accuracy. |
| 2 | Calm or mildly stir local wind and weather within a small area. |
| 3 | Call down a real gust, downpour, or bolt of lightning where none was building. |
| 4 | Summon a sustained, localized storm — wind, rain, and lightning that lasts and can be aimed. |
| 5 | Command the sky over a significant area, shaping a storm's path and intensity at will. |

### Chorus of Concord (inspired by In Nomine's Song of Harmony)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sense the emotional temperature of a room — tension, warmth, hidden hostility — at a glance. |
| 2 | Your voice and presence noticeably ease tension in people willing to be calmed. |
| 3 | Actively soothe a specific person's anger, fear, or grief in the moment, even against some resistance. |
| 4 | Mend a rift between two people who are willing, deep enough to outlast the conversation. |
| 5 | Calm or unite a crowd, turning a mob's temper or forging real consensus out of conflict. |

### Unerring Hand (inspired by Exalted's Craft Charms)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Know instantly and exactly what's wrong with a broken or flawed piece of work. |
| 2 | Work at remarkable speed without sacrificing quality, on ordinary materials and tasks. |
| 3 | Produce a single, small masterwork — flawless by any normal measure. |
| 4 | Build something that shouldn't be possible with the tools and time on hand. |
| 5 | Create a true masterwork of significant scale or complexity, finished in a fraction of the time it should take. |
