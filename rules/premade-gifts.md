# Premade Gifts

Source material and worked examples for [gifts.md](gifts.md), pulled from five White Wolf power systems in addition to the six already reviewed for the resolution mechanic (Hero System, OWoD, D&D feats/spells, Mutants & Masterminds, Trinity, Aberrant).

## Source Cross-Reference

Broad power concepts, paraphrased rather than reproduced — a source of inspiration for new Gifts, not a literal port.

- **Werewolf: The Apocalypse** (power category literally also called "Gifts", taught by spirits): heightened senses, physical enhancement (claws, speed, resilience), elemental control in small doses (wind, water, fire), fear/awe effects, camouflage, healing, refinements on shapeshifting, spirit communication, pack-coordination effects.
- **Mage: The Ascension** (Spheres — nine broad reality-affecting domains, each independently rated 1-5): matter/object transformation, life/biological manipulation, mind/thought influence, forces/energy manipulation, spirit-world interaction, time perception and manipulation, space/distance manipulation, raw magical essence, fate/probability. Of everything reviewed, Spheres are the **closest structural match** to our 5-level Gift model — each level is an explicit, escalating capability rather than a bigger number.
- **Changeling: The Dreaming** (Arts, applied to Realms): illusion/deception, object charming, nature/wild magic, movement and travel, fate-reading, dream-crafting, true-name binding.
- **Wraith: The Oblivion** (Arcanoi — also structured as 5-level power trees, another close structural match): incorporeal movement, possession of objects or the living, emotional/fear projection, fate perception, memory manipulation, spectral combat, reshaping one's own form, drawing power from the world of the living.
- **Hunter: The Reckoning** (Edges, granted by conviction rather than supernatural nature): truth-sensing, supernatural perception, resilience, minor protective wards, self-healing, righteous fury in combat, compelling honesty.

## Worked Examples

Three fully defined Gifts demonstrating the 5-level format from [gifts.md](gifts.md) — each level cumulative, each a specific new capability rather than a bigger number.

### Ember Touch (inspired by Mage's Forces Sphere)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sense heat and open flame within a room, and tell whether something recently burned. |
| 2 | Cause an existing flame to flare, dim, or shift direction; ignite something already primed to catch. |
| 3 | Conjure and sustain a small controlled flame from nothing, and shape it into simple forms. |
| 4 | Hurl a burst of true fire at a target or small area; redirect an existing large fire's spread. |
| 5 | Command fire at significant scale — bend a raging blaze to your will, or extinguish a large fire instantly. |

### Skin of the Beast (inspired by Werewolf Gifts)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | Minor animal traits bleed through — sharper senses, faint claws — subtle enough not to draw a second look. |
| 2 | Partial shift on command — claws, fangs, or fur emerge, aiding combat or environment without changing your silhouette. |
| 3 | Full transformation into one specific animal form, retaining your own mind. |
| 4 | The shift sharpens — faster transformation, plus an intermediate hybrid war-form. |
| 5 | Total mastery of the shift — instant transformation, and the ability to lend a lesser version of this Gift's traits to someone else briefly. |

### Beyond the Veil (inspired by Wraith's Arcanoi)

| Level | Capability |
|---|---|
| 1 | See and hear what lingers past death — residual impressions, faint spirits, echoes of strong emotion tied to a place. |
| 2 | Communicate with what you sense — spirits can hear and respond to you, even if no one else can perceive them. |
| 3 | Reach partly into the space between — touch, or be touched by, the intangible, briefly. |
| 4 | Cross over bodily for short periods, walking unseen and unfelt by the living. |
| 5 | Move freely between the world of the living and whatever lies beyond, and guide others across with you. |
