# Flaws

**Started 2026-08-09.** Flaws are the mirror image of [Boons](boons.md) — disadvantages, quirks, or complications a player can choose for their character, carrying forward the concept (and name, for now) from [v1's Flaws](../archive/v1/rules/flaws.md), itself built on White Wolf/World of Darkness's Merits & Flaws system.

- Structured the same way as Boons: **no levels or ranks** for most entries — a character either has a Flaw or doesn't. (Whether a Leveled/Resource-style exception belongs here too, mirroring [Resources](resources.md), is open.)
- **No numeric penalties in most cases** — a Flaw is usually a narrative complication or rule restriction, not a flat -N to some roll.
- **Taken only at character creation**, presumably in exchange for something at creation (mirroring how v1's Flaws granted bonus points) — the exact economy isn't set yet, same open status as Boons' costs.

This file is currently just a seed — **Unusual Size**, moved out of Boons, is the only entry. v1's much larger Flaw list (Physical/Mental/Cognitive/Social categories, plus Leveled Flaws like Notoriety/Enemy/Hunted) hasn't been pulled forward yet.

## Flaw List

| Flaw | Effect |
|---|---|
| Unusual Size | Notably larger or smaller than average, with the narrative tradeoffs that implies. Moved here from [Boons](boons.md) 2026-08-09 — judged to read more as a complication than an advantage. |

## Open Questions

- **The rest of v1's Flaw list** — whether to pull it forward wholesale, re-scan sources the same way Boons just did, or build it fresh, isn't decided.
- **Tiering** — Boons now use a Minor/Moderate/Major cost tier; whether Flaws should mirror that structure (a severity tier) is open. Unusual Size isn't tiered yet.
- **Flaw economy** — what a Flaw grants the character in exchange, and whether that scales with severity, is unresolved, same as it was in v1.
- **Leveled (Resource) Flaws** — v1 had Notoriety/Enemy/Hunted mirroring the Leveled (Resource) Boons now split into [Resources](resources.md); whether v2 wants the same split (a "Liabilities" file, maybe) is open.
