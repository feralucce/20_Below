# Flaws

## What a Flaw Is

Flaws are the mirror image of [Perks](perks.md) — disadvantages, quirks, or complications a player can choose for their character, directly inspired by White Wolf/World of Darkness's Merits & Flaws system.

- Structured the same way as Perks: **no levels or ranks** — a character either has a Flaw or doesn't. (Whether a subset should mirror Perks' [Leveled (Resource) Perks](perks.md#leveled-resource-perks--confirmed) exception is an open question below.)
- **No numeric penalties in most cases** — like Perks, a Flaw is usually a narrative complication or rule restriction (a locked door, not a smaller key), not a flat -N to some roll.
- **Taken only at character creation.** Flaws aren't something picked up mid-campaign or bought/traded away with XP by default (see Open Questions for exceptions).

## Relationship to Perks and Character Creation

Flaws exist as a character-creation choice, presumably granting the player something in return for accepting the disadvantage — mirroring how WoD's Merits & Flaws worked (taking a Flaw grants bonus points to spend elsewhere). The exact economy isn't set yet — see [character-creation/overview.md](../character-creation/overview.md) and Open Questions below.

## Sources Reviewed

Compiled and reorganized from the same traditions used for [Perks](perks.md#sources-reviewed), searched specifically for disadvantageous character traits, deduplicated:

- **Old World of Darkness (Flaws)** — the direct companion to the Merits reviewed for Perks, normally dotted, dots stripped here the same way.
- **Hero System (Disadvantages / Complications)** — Psychological Limitation, Physical Limitation, Social Limitation, Hunted, Rivalry, Reputation, Susceptibility, Vulnerability, Berserk, and similar. A strong source since Hero's Disadvantages are already built as "narrative complication, not a flat penalty," the same philosophy this system wants.
- **D&D 3.5e Flaws (Unearthed Arcana)** — most are pure numeric penalties (a flat -2 to a stat or AC) traded for a bonus feat, which don't fit "no numeric penalty" any more than D&D's numeric-bonus Feats fit Perks — those were **left out**. What's included below are the few whose core idea is a rule restriction or narrative complication rather than a number.

Overtly supernatural drawbacks (a curse, a bound spirit, a magical weakness) were excluded here for the same reason as Perks — they belong under a Gift's own downside (see [gifts.md](gifts.md) Open Questions on resource costs/drawbacks), not the general Flaw list.

## Flaw List

### Physical

| Flaw | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lame | Permanently reduced mobility from an old injury or condition |
| Blind / One Eye | Missing sight in one or both eyes |
| Deaf | Missing or significantly impaired hearing |
| Disfigured | A visible scar, burn, or deformity that draws stares and reactions |
| Frail | Notably weaker or more delicate than average, with the narrative tradeoffs that implies |
| Chronic Pain | A lingering injury or condition that flares up under stress or exertion |
| Addiction | Physically or psychologically dependent on a substance |
| Allergy | A specific substance or environment reliably causes a reaction |
| Slow Healer | Recovers from injury markedly slower than normal |

### Mental / Cognitive

| Flaw | Effect |
|---|---|
| Phobia | An intense, irrational fear of something specific |
| Compulsion | An urge that must be indulged, or resisted only with difficulty (gambling, hoarding, obsessive cleanliness) |
| Short Fuse | Quick to anger, prone to losing composure under provocation |
| Nightmares | Recurring dreams that disrupt rest |
| Amnesia | Missing memories from part of the character's past |
| Illiterate | Cannot read or write |
| Overconfident | Consistently underestimates danger or overestimates their own ability |
| Soft-Hearted | Struggles to act against the vulnerable, even when it costs them |
| Code of Honor | A self-imposed rule the character won't break, even when breaking it would help |

### Social

| Flaw | Effect |
|---|---|
| Rival | Someone locked in constant competition with the character |
| Secret | Something the character desperately doesn't want revealed |
| Dependent | Someone relies on the character and must be protected or provided for |
| Intolerance | Others hold a specific prejudice against the character (species, background, origin, etc.) |
| Speech Impediment | A stutter, accent, or other trait that hampers verbal communication |
| Vow | A binding oath or obligation that restricts the character's choices |

### Leveled (Resource) Flaws — confirmed

**Resolved 2026-07-14**, mirroring [Perks' Leveled (Resource) Perks](perks.md#leveled-resource-perks--confirmed): these Flaws represent a scalable liability rather than a binary yes/no trait, so they use the same **1-5 level scale as Gifts** — each level a cumulative, defined step up in severity, not a numeric roll penalty. A minor Enemy is a nuisance; a level-5 Enemy is a nemesis with real reach.

| Flaw | What Scales by Level |
|---|---|
| Notoriety | How widely and how badly the character is known — the direct mirror of the Leveled Perk Fame |
| Enemy | How capable, resourced, and motivated the specific person or group working against the character is |
| Hunted | How determined and far-reaching the pursuing authority, organization, or individual is |

**Exact per-level definitions are not yet written** — same flagged gap as the Leveled (Resource) Perks, deferred to the same future pass.

### Combat-Adjacent

Unlike Perks, these don't need to be deferred — combat mechanics already exist (see [combat.md](combat.md)) — but exact mechanical effects (if any beyond narrative restriction) aren't pinned down here, same treatment as the rest of this compiled list.

| Flaw | Effect |
|---|---|
| Berserk | Once triggered, must keep fighting at full intensity until no threats remain — can't disengage or hold back |
| Combat Paralysis | Freezes instead of acting when sudden violence breaks out |
| Code Against Killing | Refuses to use lethal force, even when it would be the easier or safer choice |
| Reduced Reactions | Slower than most to recognize and respond to danger — the mirror of Alertness/Combat Reflexes. No immunity to being [Surprised](combat.md#surprise--confirmed), and GMs may rule borderline "were they surprised?" calls against this character more readily. |
| Slow Hands | Takes longer than normal to ready a weapon — the mirror of Quick Draw |

## Open Questions

## Resolved

- **Flaw list compiled** (2026-07-14): see Flaw List above, sourced from OWoD, Hero System, and D&D 3.5e Flaws (numeric-penalty entries excluded, mirroring how Perks excluded numeric-bonus Feats).
- **Addendum: introduced Leveled (Resource) Flaws** (2026-07-14): Notoriety, Enemy, and Hunted mirror Perks' Leveled (Resource) treatment — 1-5 scale instead of binary. See Leveled (Resource) Flaws above.
- **Flaw economy confirmed** (2026-07-14): taking a Flaw grants the player extra build points to spend elsewhere **at character creation only** — confirms Flaws aren't picked up mid-campaign (already stated above) and, since the exchange is a one-time creation-time transaction, **a Flaw is fixed for the character's lifetime once chosen** — there's no mechanism to buy one off later. By the same "cost scales with strength" logic already established for Perks, **the points granted should scale with a Flaw's severity** rather than being uniform — flagged as inferred by symmetry, not separately confirmed by exact numbers, which wait on the broader economy (see Open Questions).
