# Boons

**Renamed 2026-08-09** from v1's "Perks," to fit the fated-heroes vocabulary already established elsewhere (Wyrd, Ki, Fate Tokens, Warp/Weft) instead of a generic mechanical term. Content carried forward from [v1's Perks](../archive/v1/rules/perks.md), reorganized — not yet re-costed or re-mechanized for v2 (see [Open Questions](#open-questions)).

## What a Boon Is

Boons are a **grab bag** — small advantages that don't fit under Skills (learned, practiced competence, see [skills.md](skills.md)) or the not-yet-built Powers system (truly exceptional/supernatural capability). Boons:

- **Have no levels or ranks.** A character either has a Boon or doesn't — there's no 1-10 scale, no dots, no tiers. (The old resource-type entries that *did* scale — Wealth, Contacts, Fringe Benefit, etc. — have been split out entirely; see [resources.md](resources.md).)
- **Do not grant numerical bonuses to rolls.** A Boon is usually a rule exception, an unlocked narrative option, or a standing fact about the character's body or reputation — not a flat +N to some roll.

Boons have a mirror-image counterpart carried forward conceptually from v1's Flaws — character-creation disadvantages built the same way (no levels, mostly non-numeric). Not yet renamed or re-adapted for v2.

**Organized by cost tier** — Minor, Moderate, Major — same three tiers v1 used, alphabetized within each. **Combat Reflexes and Quick Draw have been dropped** (2026-08-09): both leaned entirely on v1-specific mechanics (Initiative Degree, Action Economy) that v2 either lacks or has replaced, and neither survived the cut to v2 shape. **Alertness stays**, still flagged — it references v1's Surprise mechanic, not yet rebuilt in v2, but the Boon itself (immunity to being Surprised) is a clean enough concept to carry forward and rebuild against once Surprise exists.

**Expanded 2026-08-09** with candidates pulled from a source scan — GURPS's mundane Advantages, Chronicles of Darkness Merits, D&D 5e's non-magical feats, and Hero System Talents — chosen for fitting "clearly more than an average person, not remotely magical" and, for Major especially, for actually reshaping how a character handles a whole category of situation rather than just being a flavorful flat trait. Each new entry below is tagged with what it's adapted from. **Not yet reviewed or costed against v2's economy** — same caveat as everything else in this file.

## Minor

| Boon | Effect |
|---|---|
| Ambidextrous | No penalty for using your off-hand. |
| Double-Jointed | Escape bonds/tight spaces, contort with ease. |
| Encyclopedic Knowledge | Broad, ready trivia and general knowledge on nearly any topic — distinct from Eidetic Memory's perfect recall of things you actually saw, read, or heard. *(Chronicles of Darkness: Encyclopedic Knowledge)* |
| Feign Death | Convincingly fool onlookers — and a casual medical check — into believing you're dead or unconscious, on your own choice. *(Hero System: Simulate Death)* |
| Higher Purpose | Others innately sense you're meant for something greater. |
| Innate Compass | Always know true north and roughly what time it is, no matter where you are. *(GURPS: Absolute Direction; Chronicles of Darkness: Direction Sense)* |
| Iron Stomach | Eat or drink almost anything without illness. |
| Light Sleeper | Wake instantly at the slightest disturbance. |
| Lightning Calculator | Instant complex mental math. |
| Mimicry | Convincingly imitate voices and sounds you've heard. *(D&D 5e: Actor; Hero System: Mimicry)* |
| Natural Linguist | Pick up new languages unusually fast. |
| Patient | Unshakeable pacing — you can't be rushed or goaded into acting before you're ready. *(Chronicles of Darkness: Patient)* |
| Speed Reading | Read or skim at exceptional speed. |

## Moderate

| Boon | Effect |
|---|---|
| Alertness | Immune to being Surprised while conscious, unless under a mind-altering effect that would specifically allow it. (References v1's Surprise mechanic — not yet rebuilt in v2.) |
| Closed Book | Your emotions and tells are unreadable — others can't read you the way they can read most people. *(Chronicles of Darkness: Closed Book)* |
| Common Sense | The GM will give you a heads-up before you commit to an obviously terrible decision. |
| Concentration | Impervious to being distracted once focused on a task. |
| Danger Sense | A "bad feeling" before danger strikes — the GM gives you a warning. |
| Eidetic Memory | Perfect recall of anything seen, read, or heard. |
| Enchanting Voice | Your voice alone holds attention and charms. |
| Eye for the Strange | You notice when something supernatural is going on around you well before anyone untrained would. Fits the setting's fated-heroes premise the same way Occult does on the Skill side. *(Chronicles of Darkness: Eye for the Strange)* |
| Fleet of Foot | Noticeably faster on foot than the effort should allow. |
| Iron Will | Notably resistant to fear, intimidation, and manipulation. |
| Prestigious Name | An inherited or earned reputation that opens doors before you say a word. |
| Rapid Healing | Wounds that would sideline anyone else for weeks close in days. *(GURPS: Rapid Healing)* |
| Steadfast Presence | Allies who can see or hear you find it easier to hold their nerve, just from your presence. *(D&D 5e: Inspiring Leader, reframed as a passive presence rather than an activated buff)* |

## Major

Previously empty (Combat Reflexes was the sole Major-tier Boon in v1 and was dropped — see above). Filled 2026-08-09 with five candidates, each deliberately reshaping a whole category of situation rather than granting a flat bonus:

| Boon | Effect |
|---|---|
| Living Legend | Your reputation precedes you at a civilization-spanning scale — whole factions, cities, or movements already have an opinion of you before you arrive. A bigger sibling to Prestigious Name. *(Chronicles of Darkness: Status, top dots; Savage Worlds: Legendary-rank reputation fiction)* |
| Regenerator | Your body repairs itself at a rate that shouldn't be biologically possible — injuries that would scar or maim anyone else heal clean. *(GURPS: Regeneration, toned down from its usual supernatural framing to a mundane extreme)* |
| Trained by a Master | Name one Skill: it performs at a level ordinary training can't reach. Once per session, a failure on that Skill can be treated as a success instead. *(GURPS: Trained By A Master)* |
| Unbreakable Will | Full immunity to domination, possession, and mind control. You can still be deceived, but never puppeted. *(GURPS: Unfazeable; Chronicles of Darkness: Unbreakable)* |
| Unstoppable | **Renamed and mechanized 2026-08-09** (was Indomitable). Being reduced to 0 [Health Levels](core-mechanic.md#health-levels) doesn't render you unconscious — you keep going into negative Health Levels, down to the same −(Health sub-stat) death threshold everyone eventually hits, instead of blacking out at 0. While in that negative range: (1) you're extremely hurt but conscious — you can talk, move, and act at rest with no restriction; (2) any exertion — combat, strenuous effort, or moving quickly — costs **1 Ki** per instance. *(GURPS: Hard to Kill, replaced with a mechanic built directly on this system's own Health Level/Ki structure rather than loose pulp-hero fiction)* |

## Open Questions

- **Cost/pricing not yet re-derived for v2.** v1 priced Boons on a 3-tier creation-pool cost (Minor 1 / Moderate 2 / Major 3) and a `tier × 3` XP cost to acquire one after creation — none of that has been re-examined against v2's actual character-creation point economy, which isn't built yet.
- **Alertness references v1's Surprise mechanic**, which v2 hasn't rebuilt. Its effect needs restating once Surprise exists in v2 terms.
- **Flaws' v2 name and adaptation** — not addressed here; still carries v1's name and shape.
- **A Major-tier Boon** — the tier is currently empty; whether that's fine long-term or something should eventually fill it is open.
