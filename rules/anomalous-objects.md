# Anomalous Objects

Nothing here occurs in nature. Every one of these was made - by somebody,
by something, or by an event that nobody has managed to explain since.
They turn up in evidence lockers and estate sales and the back of a
drawer in a house whose owner died badly. They are not bought. A
character has one because a GM decided they do, or because they spent a
[Signature Possession](resources.md#the-resource-list) on it.

**Level 1 to 5**, the same ladder every Resource uses. The Level says how
much the thing does and how much it costs to wake up.

| Level | Ki to activate |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | 1 |
| 3-4 | 2 |
| 5 | 3 |

**Spend the Ki and it works.** There is no attunement, no ritual, no
roll to see whether the object cooperates. It is yours, you are holding
it, and it does the thing. What it costs you is Ki you now do not have
for anything else, which is the whole of the price.

That table is the default, not a law. A few objects charge differently -
by the die, by the use, by something worse - and where they do, the entry
says so instead.

Every entry says what the thing physically is, and whether it has to be
**held** or **worn**. Unless an entry says otherwise, an activation lasts
the **Scene**.

**Some of them break.** An object marked **Fragile** was not built to
last, and its entry says which of two ways it goes.

- **Charges.** It has a set number of uses. Spend the last one and the
  object is finished.
- **A roll.** After every use, roll **2d10** against your **Klotho + 10**.
  Fail and it breaks in your hands.

Broken is broken. Nothing here repairs one, nobody is making more, and
the thing that was in it does not go anywhere you can follow.

When a character's Gift lives inside one of these rather than inside
them, that is the [Item of Power](flaws.md#item-of-power) Flaw, and the
Flaw's rules apply on top of everything here.

## The List

### Amulet of Recognition

**Level 2. 1 Ki. Worn.**

A flat disc of tarnished silver on a broken watch chain, a shallow spiral
worn nearly smooth by handling. It sits warm against the skin whether or
not anyone has been holding it.

For the rest of the Scene, you know which people are touched by fate. You
can tell if a person or animal is Gifted, or non-human.

### Mourning Veil

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Worn.**

Black lace on a tortoiseshell comb, stiff with age and smelling faintly
of camphor. The stitching is by hand, and it does not match any pattern
anybody has managed to place. It goes over the face.

For the rest of the Scene, you read as a hole in the water. No Gift or
Anomalous Object can detect you as anything other than human.

### Tanner's Coat

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Worn.**

A hide jacket, badly cured and never quite dry, with a smell that people
notice and then decide not to mention. Whatever it was made from had more
ribs than it should have.

For the rest of the Scene, nothing that is not alive can touch you.
Things with bodies hit you normally; things without them cannot land a
hand, a tooth, or a cold grip on you at all.

### Hunter's Mask

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Worn.**

The front half of an animal's skull, cut and strapped to fit a human
face. Nobody agrees on what the animal was. The teeth are still in it.

For the rest of the Scene you take Advantage on any Perception roll made
by sight, hearing or smell. Anyone who looks directly at your face takes
Disadvantage on their next roll - not because the mask is frightening,
but because you are while wearing it.

### Shed Skin

**Level 2. 1 Ki. Worn.**

A long coat stitched from something translucent and papery, like a
snake's shed laid flat and cut to a pattern. It crackles when you put it
on and goes silent the moment it is settled.

You aren't hidden - people just cannot see you. At all. They
unconsciously avoid looking at you and will avoid where you are. The
instant you move - everyone can see you. Your sudden appearance is sure
to be noticed.

### Unicorn Horn Cup

**Level 2. 1 Ki. Held.**

A shallow cup turned from a single piece of spiraled horn and banded in
tarnished silver. It is far too light for its size, and it is always
slightly warmer than the room.

Anything drunk from it cannot hurt you. Poison, drugs, contaminated
water, whatever somebody stirred into it while you were looking the other
way. You still taste it. It simply does not do anything.

### Dagger of Thorns

**Level 2. 1 Ki. Held.**

A thin knife with a blade too dark for steel, its grip wound in dried
bramble with the thorns still on it. Holding it costs you a little blood
every time, and those cuts are always closed by morning.

For the rest of the Scene, anyone who costs you a Health Level loses one
of their own. It does not matter how far away they are, whether you saw
them, or whether they ever work out what happened to them.

### Nightjar

**Level 2. 1 Ki. Held.**

A small stoppered jar of clouded glass with a single grey feather sealed
inside it. The feather moves when the jar does not.

Touch it to the forehead of someone who is asleep and steal one dream out
of their psyche. You see it the way they were seeing it, all of it,
including the parts they would not have told you. They never have that
dream again and will not remember ever having had it.

### Backglass

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

A hand mirror silvered on the wrong side, so you look into the glass
rather than at it. The frame is hewn from bone and always warm to the
touch.

Hold it up in a room and pick any hour from the last day. You see what
happened there. The mirror does not transmit sound and nothing seen in
the glass can perceive you.

### Dagger of Teeth

**Level 5. 3 Ki. Held.**

A blade knapped from a single tooth, far larger than anything living has
any business growing, seated in a handle of black wood. It never warms
up.

For the rest of the Scene, Health Levels this knife takes do not come
back on their own. No rest returns them. They need stitches or the
effects of supernatural healing to heal. Every one of them leaves a
Scar.

### Stormbound Cloak

**Level 4. 2 Ki. Worn.**

Overlapping feathers in shades of blue, grey and white, sewn in rows onto
old canvas. They crackle with static electricity.

For the rest of the Scene you can fly, at your usual Movement Rate. You
are not fast, you are not quiet, and everybody who looks up sees exactly
what you are.

### Silverfire Weapon

**Level 5. 1 Ki per die. Held.**

Silver, too soft to hold an edge and holding one anyway, cut along its
whole length with work so fine it reads as texture until you look
closely, and then it reads as writing. Nobody has translated any of it.

**The GM fixes its shape when the character is given it, and the shape
never changes after.** Sword, axe, spear, hammer, maul, whip, bow, knife.
Use the [weapon table](weapons.md#basic-weapons) entry for whatever shape
it took: that is what it weighs, what it reaches, and what Skill swings
it.

When you land an attack, spend a point of Ki per die and the damage that
die does is unsoakable. It is per attack, every attack, and it throttles
fast.

### Magpie's Fanny Pack

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Worn.**

A nylon fanny pack in a colourway that was ugly when it was new. The zip
runs the whole way around and then keeps going about an inch further than
the pack is wide.

For the rest of the Scene it holds anything you can push through the zip,
up to about as much as would fit in the trunk of a car, and it weighs
nothing no matter what is in there.

It spits things out in whatever order chance sees fit. You always get
something that you put into it, but rarely the thing you were looking
for.

Roll **2d10** every time you reach in.

- **A roll of 2**: you get exactly what you were looking for.
- **Your [Lucky Number](rules.md#lucky-number)**: you pull out something
  nobody put in there.
- **Anything else**: something of yours, at random.

If one roll manages both, the pack is being funny. You get what you
wanted, and something else besides.

### Eclipse Glasses

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Worn.**

Aviators with lenses dark enough to look solid from the outside. They
came in a hard case with somebody else's name written inside the lid in
marker, and the name is not one anybody has been able to trace.

For the rest of the Scene you cannot be Blinded. Not by a flashbang, not
by pepper spray, not by anything that works by making a person shut their
eyes. It is difficult to get a read on you as well, and every roll made
to do it takes Disadvantage.

### Plain Sight

**Level 5. 3 Ki. Worn.**

A plain brass coat button on a loop of thread, worn smooth on one face.
There is nothing remarkable about it, and there is nothing remarkable
about it in a way that starts to bother you after a while.

For the rest of the Scene, no bystander without a Gift sees anything
supernatural. Not you, not what you are fighting, not whatever came
through the wall. Everyone present looks like a person and everything
present looks like something that could be in the room.

It does not hide actions. Whatever gets done still gets done in front of
them and they will remember all of it. They will simply remember people
doing it.

### Shadow Bangle

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Worn.**

A thin band of dark metal that takes no shine at all, whatever you do to
it. The shadow it casts is a little longer than it should be and always
falls away from you.

For the rest of the Scene you take Advantage on every Stealth roll made
in anything less than full daylight. You do not need cover, a shadow, or
anywhere in particular to stand. The dark comes with you and there is
always enough of it.

You are not invisible. It hides you from anyone looking for you, not from
anyone already looking at you.

### White Wing

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

A partridge's wing, dried whole and still jointed, every feather unbroken
and white. It hangs from a gold ring on a short chain, and the chain is
much older than the wing.

Name one thing a person saw, heard or worked out, then walk a circle
around them counter-clockwise three times with the wing low enough to
touch the ground. It takes about a minute, and they have to let you do it
or be in no position to stop you.

That memory is gone. Not suppressed, not blurred. They do not have a gap
where it was and will not notice anything missing, because as far as they
are concerned the evening simply had one less thing in it. It never comes
back.

### Second Face

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Worn.**

A small gold pendant, thin as a fingernail and slightly convex, with
nothing engraved on it. Held at the right angle it reflects a face that is
not yours, and never the same one twice.

Pick something you are not. A different person, an empty stairwell, a
colleague nobody thinks about. For the rest of the Scene that is what you
are, to the eye, the ear and the nose alike. You sound like it. You smell
like it. A camera records the false face rather than yours.

Anyone who touches you touches you. Whatever they were about to believe,
they stop believing immediately.

### Unbroken Cord

**Level 4. 2 Ki. Held.**

A length of hemp cord, knotted along its whole length in a pattern that
does not repeat and has no beginning. Hold both ends and there is still
more cord between your hands than there should be.

Name one Skill and one Gift of yours and spend the Ki. For the rest of
the Scene you do not have them. Not reduced, not at Disadvantage. Gone.

Whoever is wearing the cord around their neck has both instead, at your
rating, as though they had belonged to that person all along. Take it off
and hand it over and they go with the cord.

### Dead Phone

**Level 4. Held.**

A phone about fifteen years out of date, scuffed, with a cracked screen
and no SIM. Nobody who has owned it has ever charged it and the battery
has never been below full.

**Spend any amount of Ki into it at any time, up to 5.** It keeps what you
put in indefinitely, through rests, through Months, through anything.
Anyone holding the phone can draw that Ki back out and spend it as their
own.

It also rings. Not often, and never with a number. Whatever is on the
line knows things, answers about one question in three honestly, and has
wanted something from you since the first time you picked it up.

### Tessera

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

A solid of flat faces, about the size of a die, that is a different solid
each time you look at it. Nobody has counted the faces twice and got the
same number.

Name one kind of thing. Not a person: a kind. The dead, the things that
live in the walls, whatever has been following your friend home.

For the rest of the Scene every one of them reads you as one of their
own. They will not attack you unprovoked, they will talk to you the way
they talk to each other, and they will expect you to already know things.
They do not become friendly, and nothing stops them noticing that you are
behaving oddly for one of them.

### Silk Glove

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Worn.**

A glove woven from spider silk, grey, seamless, and warm inside no matter
the weather. It fits whoever puts it on, and nobody has ever found the
end of the thread.

For the rest of the Scene you can draw web out of your closed hand, as
much as you want, faster than a person can back away from it.

Throwing it at something is an attack roll against that target's Defense.
On a hit they are Restrained and stay Restrained until they break out
with a Potence check against your Klotho. Anchor a line to two points
instead and it holds a person's weight: across a gap, up a wall, or
across a doorway at ankle height where nobody looks.

### Hearts of Midnight

**Level 5. No Ki. Worn.**

Three rough black stones threaded on copper wire. The stones seem to be
lit from an external source. No external light source has any effect
whatsoever.

While you wear them, no Gift and no Anomalous Object can affect you. Not
yours, not anybody's, not one already running when you put them on.

While you wear them you cannot spend Ki. Not on Gifts, not on an object,
not to hold a Health Level, not for anything.

Take them off and everything works again, and everything can reach you
again. Deciding when to do that is the whole of it.

### The Laminate

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

An ID card, soft at the corners from a lanyard, with your photograph on
it. Everything else printed on it is different every time somebody reads
it, and not one of them has ever noticed that.

Show it and it is exactly the credential that person needed to see.
Badge, warrant card, contractor's pass, clearance, work order, next of
kin. They believe it, and they let you through.

It does not survive being typed into anything. The moment a name off that
card goes into a system that can check it there is no such person, there
never was, and whoever typed it in is now looking at you.

### Blue Box

**Level 4. 2 Ki. Held.**

Hand-soldered electronics packed into an old cigarette tin, one button,
and a speaker far too loud for its size. What it plays is a tone nobody
can hold in their head afterwards, though everyone agrees they would know
it again.

For the rest of the Scene, anything you could reasonably call an
electronic lock opens for you. Doors, phones, vehicles, accounts,
cameras, the whole building. No roll, no tools, and nothing in the logs,
because there is nothing in the logs to find.

Everything it opens stays open. It does not close behind you and you
cannot close it.

### The Collar

**Level 5. 3 Ki. Worn.**

A wide leather collar with a sealed plastic housing riveted to it, of the
sort a wildlife service uses. There is no receiver. Nobody has ever found
the receiver.

Put it on an animal and you know where that animal is, exactly, for as
long as it wears it. Distance does not matter and neither does anything
in the way.

Put it on a person and you know that too. They cannot take it off. Nobody
who did not put it on them can take it off. While it is on them they take
Disadvantage on every Social roll they make, because it is visible, it is
obviously not medical, and everyone can see it.

### The Long Coat

**Level 4. 1 Ki per item. Worn.**

A large black overcoat, well worn, slightly too big for whoever has it
on. The inside pockets are the wrong shape and there are more of them
each time you look.

Reach inside and name one ordinary object small enough to conceal in a
coat. Spend the Ki and roll **2d10** against **Klotho + Difficulty**,
with the GM setting the [Difficulty](rules.md#difficulty-chart) by how
unlikely the object is. Today's newspaper is Trivial. The key to one
specific door is Nearly Impossible.

On a success it is in your hand. On a failure it is not in there, it was
never in there, and that object cannot be asked for again tonight.

You can hold out as many objects at once as your **Klotho**. Past that,
something goes back in before anything else comes out.

### Black Arrows

**Level 1. 1 Ki per shot. Fragile, 3 charges. Held.**

Three arrows with heads knapped from black glass, carried in a quiver
that does not match them. The shafts are always warm and the glass never
is.

Loose one from any bow and spend the Ki. It does not need an attack roll
and it does not miss, provided you have laid eyes or ears on that target
at some point this Scene. Cover does not stop it, darkness does not stop
it, and it will go around a corner to reach something that has just
broken line of sight.

Anything that is not natural cannot hide the wound, and cannot pretend to
be something else while the arrow is still lodged in it.

Each arrow is one charge. When they are gone you will not find more.

### Grave Dust

**Level 1. 1 Ki per question. Fragile, 5+1d10 charges. Held.**

A screw-top jar of grey powder, about a handful. It smells like a cellar.

Open the jar and sprinkle some of the dust over someone dead less than a
day and ask them a question. They answer in their own voice, out of their
own mouth, and they do not lie, because there is nothing left in them
that wants anything.

They only know what they knew. Most people do not know who killed them,
and almost nobody knows why.

When you obtain the jar, it has enough dust for 5+1d10 questions.

### Liquid Courage

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1 charge. Held.**

A dented steel flask of something amber and far too sweet, always warm
however cold the night is. Everybody who drinks from it says it tastes
like something they had once and cannot place.

Drink it and take **3 Fate Tokens** immediately, over your usual cap.
These Fate Tokens don't count against the number of tokens you can spend.
For the rest of the Scene nothing can frighten you, and afterwards you
will not be sure whether that was the mead or you.

Any of those Tokens still unspent at the end of the Scene are gone.

### Moonmoss

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1 charge. Held.**

A handful of pale moss in a jam jar, dry to the touch, giving off about
as much light as a full moon.

Crack the jar and for the rest of the night it shows you what is there.
It reveals anything hiding via supernatural means.

Nobody without a Gift sees the light at all. To them you are searching a
dark room with an empty jar in your hands.

### Hollow Bone

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 5 charges. Held.**

A length of hollow bone about as long as a finger, polished smooth by
handling, with a hole at each end and nothing inside it.

Put it between your teeth. For the rest of the Scene you breathe,
whatever you are breathing in. Water, smoke, tear gas, a sealed room, a
room with nothing in it at all.

It does nothing about pressure, cold, or anything in the water that has
taken an interest in you. It only ever solves breathing.

### The Bats

**Level 1. 1 Ki per flight. Fragile, 6 charges. Held.**

Three small bats, long dead and dried with their wings folded, in a tin
lined with newspaper from a year nobody has checked.

Throw one and it opens up and goes. It searches wherever you pointed it
and comes back to your hand. A building of any size takes it about ten
minutes.

What it gives you arrives all at once and is not a report. It is the
place, the way a bat had it: shapes, distances, where the gaps are, which
rooms have something breathing in them and roughly where that thing is
standing. It does not do faces, writing, or colour.

Each bat flies twice. When all three have gone twice, what is in the tin
is three dead bats.

### Bone Whistle

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1d10+3 charges. Held.**

A whistle cut from a wolf's legbone, worn shiny at the mouthpiece by
somebody else.

Blow it and every Gifted thing within about five miles hears it, knows
roughly where it came from, and knows what it means. No ordinary person
hears anything at all.

It does not ask for friends specifically. Whoever is closest is who hears
it loudest.

### Long Whispers

**Level 1. 1 Ki per use. Fragile, 1d10+3 charges. Worn.**

A single gold earring, a plain hoop, warm. Whoever wore it before you had
the other ear pierced.

Name a person you have met and spend the Ki. You either hear whatever
they are saying right now, wherever they are, for about a minute, or you
say one sentence into their ear from wherever you are. Not both.

They hear it in your voice. They have no way to answer and no way to tell
whether you are still listening.

### Test Vial

**Level 1. 1 Ki per test. Fragile, 6 charges. Held.**

A short glass vial with a cork, the glass slightly green, in a card
sleeve with a column of ticks on it and no writing.

Put a sample in and spend the Ki. Water, blood, a scraping off a wall, a
pill, whatever somebody handed you in a glass. It goes clear if the
sample is exactly what it looks like, and it goes black if it is not.

Black covers everything. Poison, disease, contamination, and whatever it
is when a thing has been somewhere it should not have been. It never
tells you which.

### Moon Sign

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1 charge. Held.**

A flat clay crescent about the size of a palm, unfired, with a mark
pressed into one face by a thumb that was not a person's.

Press it into the ground, a doorframe, a floor, a wall. For the rest of
the night nothing unnatural crosses that line unless somebody inside
invites it, and everything unnatural knows the line is there from a long
way off.

It does not stop people. Anyone who means you harm and happens to be
human walks straight in.

### Black Scale

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1 charge. Held.**

A single scale the size of a playing card, black and oil-slick, shed by
something that is still alive somewhere. It is unpleasant to hold for
reasons nobody can put a finger on.

Crush it. Everything within a couple of hundred meters that is unnatural
lights up to you, all at once: where it is, what it is attached to, and
how long it has been like that. Walls do not stop it.

Then it is in you. For the rest of the Scene anything that can tell the
natural from the unnatural reads you as unnatural, including anything
that hunts by it.

### Nightmare Coins

**Level 1. 1 Ki per coin. Fragile, 1d10+3 charges. Held.**

A small stack of coins in no currency anybody recognises, worn thin, with
markings on both faces that are not quite the same as each other.

Throw one down where people can see it. Everyone looking at it rolls
**2d10** against their **Psyche + 5**. Anyone who fails cannot act for a
minute, and spends it somewhere else entirely, seeing something that is
not in the room.

### Silver Water

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1d10/3 charges rounded up. Held.**

A sealed bottle of water with nothing wrong with it. It is always cool
and refreshing, no matter the temperature of the room.

Drink it and sleep. You awake and one Scar has been removed. You remember
how you got it.

### Black Cone

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Fragile, 1d10/3 charges rounded up. Held.**

A cone of black incense marbled with gold, in a paper sleeve gone soft
with age. It smells of nothing at all until it is lit.

Burn it where people are sleeping. Everyone asleep in the building spends
the night in the same dream, together, and knows they are. They can talk,
and what they agree on holds.

It does not wake anybody, choose who is included, or end early. Anyone
who falls asleep in that building before dawn joins whatever is already
going on in there.

### Sanctuary Chimes

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

Five short brass tubes on fishing line, the kind that hangs on a porch.
Four of them ring. The fifth never has.

Hang them somewhere and spend the Ki. For a full day, you know the moment
anything unnatural comes within about a hundred meters of them, from
wherever you are and whether or not you are asleep. You know which
direction and roughly how far from the chimes they are.

The fifth tube rings if the thing is already inside.

### The Asking Whistle

**Level 5. 3 Ki. Held.**

A whistle carved from something pale that is neither bone nor wood.
Blowing it makes a sound nobody has ever described the same way twice.

Blow it and something comes. Not a person, not an animal. It arrives
inside a minute, it is never the same thing twice, and the GM decides
what it is.

It will do one thing for you, once, and it will do it properly. Then it
goes. It remembers you, and the next time you blow the whistle it is
likelier to be that one again.

### Truth Earring

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Worn.**

A small silver hoop, plain, slightly out of round. It is cold against the
ear and gets colder as the evening goes on.

For the rest of the Scene it gets warm when somebody in earshot says
something they know to be untrue. It does not say who and it does not say
what.

It tells you nothing about who is wrong, who is repeating a lie they
believe, or who is telling you a truth designed to leave you with
entirely the wrong idea. Those are the ones that get people killed.

### Wind Whistle

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

A whistle of grey stone with three holes bored through it, one of which
does not go anywhere. It is always cold and there is always a faint
draught from it, indoors, in a sealed room, in a bag.

For the rest of the Scene the wind does what you tell it. Not weather.
Wind: strong enough to clear a room of smoke or gas in a round, to take a
scent from where you are standing to somewhere a mile away, to make a
fire jump a firebreak, or to put a person off their feet if they were not
braced.

It's loud, it's obnoxious, and everybody outdoors for half a mile notices
the weather doing something it was not doing a second ago.

### Pan Pipes of Harmony

**Level 3. 2 Ki. Held.**

A short rank of pale reed pipes bound with waxed cord, no maker's mark on
any of them. They are slightly warm, and they are in tune with whatever
is already playing.

Play them. For as long as you keep playing and nothing interrupts you,
anybody who wants to do violence to anybody must first roll **2d10**
against **their Presence + (10 - your Presence)**. On a failure they do
not, and they will have a reason for it that satisfies them.

They roll once per round, and once somebody gets through it they are
through it for the rest of the Scene. They still cannot come at you. For
as long as the pipes are playing, whoever is playing them is the one
person in the room nobody can raise a hand to.

They do nothing about violence already underway. Once the first punch
lands you are a person standing in a fight playing a set of pipes.

### Horn of Distress

**Level 1. 1 Ki. Held.**

A ram's horn, cracked along one side and bound with wire that has been
there longer than the crack. It is heavier at the wrong end.

Blow it and everyone who has ever heard you blow it knows, wherever they
are, that it was you, that you are in trouble now, and where you are. No
ordinary person hears anything.

If nobody has heard you blow it before, nothing happens at all. It only
ever reaches people who already know the sound of you needing help.
