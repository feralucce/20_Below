{%- comment -%} The site navigation. One copy, included everywhere. Paths are absolute so this works identically at the root, in /rules/ and in /docs/. The site is served from the domain root (CNAME 20belowrpg.com) and the project-pages URL redirects there. A page marks its own section by setting `nav_section` in its front matter - one of: start, options, combat, gm, downloads, license. The matching top-level item gets aria-current, so a reader can see where they are. Omitting it is fine; nothing is then marked. Styles live in /docs/assets/css/site-nav.css. The toggle behaviour is in /docs/assets/js/site-nav.js. The skip link ahead of the nav is the first thing a keyboard or screen reader reaches on any page, and jumps past ~40 nav links to the page's own
. It is invisible until focused. {%- endcomment -%} Skip to content
