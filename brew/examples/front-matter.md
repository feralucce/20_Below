## What you need

Two ten-sided dice per player. Something to write on. Three or four
people and an evening.

Everything else in this book is optional in the way a map is optional.

## How to read this book

**Rolls.** Every roll in this book is written the same way: roll 2d10 and
try to come in at or under a target. Low is good. Where a rule needs a
specific number of dice it says so.

**Bold** marks a term that means something exact. Every one of them is in
the Glossary at the back, and the first use in a chapter says where the
full rule lives.

*Italics* is emphasis, or something being said out loud.

**Page references** look like this: see **Damage**, page 000. If you are
reading a PDF, they are links.
