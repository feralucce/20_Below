Player's Quick Reference

Roll 2d10 under Element + Difficulty. Low is good.

2 - both dice showing 1 - is a critical success, whatever the target was.

20 - both showing 10 - is a catastrophic failure. The attempt fails, something else goes wrong on top of it, and in a fight you are Off Balance until the end of your next turn.

Advantage and Disadvantage

Advantage - roll 3d10, keep the lowest two

Disadvantage - roll 3d10, keep the highest two

Both are binary - three sources are worth exactly one. They cancel one for one, and whatever is left over is what you get. They belong to the core roll only, never to damage dice.

In a Fight

The round

Roll Initiative, declare your Action Bracket, act in order. Fast goes first, Slow goes last.

BracketActions
Fast1You go first, and that is all you do
Normal2A move and an attack, usually
Slow1You go last. Called shots allowed, and you attack at Advantage

Spend 1 Ki per step to bump the Bracket you declared - Slow to Normal, or Normal to Fast.

Attacking

Step one - 2d10 under Element + Difficulty vs. their Defense. Roll low.

Step two - your Damage in d10, each die separately against their Wall. Roll high.

The WallsStops
SoakPhysical attacks
PresenceSocial attacks
PsycheMental attacks

Ki Infusion - roll the damage dice first, then spend 1 Ki per die to add your matching sub-stat to that die. Ferocity for Physical, Presence for Social, Psyche for Mental.
