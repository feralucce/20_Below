Project Management Portfolio

A film producer is a project manager, just a specialized one. You hold what the thing is supposed to be and what it's actually going to take at the same time, and you keep both honest. I did that for fourteen years in independent film and television. I've spent the last year doing it on 20 Below, as the hub for a fifteen-person team with an AI assistant in the loop.

On these productions that was me. Most of the time I was also writing, directing, shooting or cutting, so the schedule and the budget had nobody else to land on.
