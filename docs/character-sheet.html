THE CORE ROLL

Roll 2d10 and sum. Success on equal or lower than Attribute + Difficulty — Difficulty runs 0 (nearly impossible) to 10 (trivial). 2 always succeeds. 20 always fails. Untrained rolls use Difficulty alone.

Every Skill defaults to an Element. Challenge that default by naming one of your Descriptors.

RECOVERY

Short Rest — about an hour, one per night. Heal the matching sub-stat ÷ 2 (min 1) on Health, Sanity and Poise; Ki by Klotho ÷ 2; drop one Exhausted level.
Full Night's Rest — all three tracks and Ki to full, Exhausted cleared. Below 0, any rest returns only 1.
