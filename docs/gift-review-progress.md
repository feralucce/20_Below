This also prompted a new system-wide rule: **[Flat DoS/DoF bonus steps](../rules/core-mechanic.md#degree-of-success--degree-of-failure)** — escalating Gift/Perk bonuses now formally step through 1 → 3 → 5, not an arbitrary sequence (walked back from an initial +2/+5/+8 draft that was "obscene").

## Full-catalog concrete-mechanics pass, completed 2026-07-16

Every one of the 61 Gifts in the catalog now has, per level: a **bolded, named sub-ability**
and a real mechanic tied to the system's existing vocabulary instead of narrative-only
prose. Ruleset governing this pass lives in [flavor-writing-style.md](flavor-writing-style.md)
(Gift-by-Gift Concrete-Mechanics Review Ruleset section, added this session). Design
decisions made along the way, now written into that ruleset so future Gift work stays
consistent:

- **Soak stays Bulwark-exclusive**, per [core-mechanic.md](../rules/core-mechanic.md#calculated-defensive-traits).
  Every other Gift's damage-resistance effect uses the DoS-penalty convention instead
  (attacks against the target suffer -1/-3/-5 DoS), which is why **Undying Fury** ended up
  with DoS steps rather than a second Soak source.
- **Offensive Gifts deal damage via the Weapon Base table** ([combat.md](../rules/combat.md#damage--confirmed)):
  Level 3 = **Base +3** (Light), Level 4 = **Base +6** (Martial), Level 5 = **Base +9**
  (Ordnance, can spill across Health Levels), resolved as an Attack vs. **Defense**, not
  Resolve, since it's physical damage. This corrected several Elemental & Energy and Sound
  Gifts that had been resolving offensive effects against Resolve, the wrong defensive
  trait for a physical attack.
- **Living Mend and Borrowed Vitality now tie explicitly to Health Levels** (recover 1 per
  night/scene/action escalating by level, or drain 1-2 from a source) — the first place in
  the ruleset that a concrete healing/recovery rate has been defined; [combat.md](../rules/combat.md)
  still flags the general Healing mechanic as undesigned, so this may need reconciling
  with whatever that ends up being.
- **One overlap surfaced and was resolved during the pass**: Worldwalker (Cosmic/Divine)
  vs. Step Between and Through the Cracks (both Movement & Spatial) all cover "get from A
  to B unconventionally." Kept separate with a differentiation footnote (same pattern as
  Storm's Herald/Voltaic Pulse) rather than merged, since Worldwalker needs neither sight
  nor familiarity with the destination, which is also why it's gated to the rare tier.
- A few Gifts (Iron Lungs, Adaptive Flesh, Undying Vigor, and similar pure environmental/
  survival permission Gifts) got bolded sub-ability names but **no invented numbers** —
  they don't compete against anything, so a number would be decoration, not mechanics.
- The four Cosmic/Divine reality-warping Gifts (Voice of Creation, Unerring Hand, Mantle
  of the Divine, Worldwalker) got bolded names but were deliberately **not** forced into
  numeric combat mechanics at every level — reality-bending effects are intentionally
  GM-narrated per [core-mechanic.md's Critical Results](../rules/core-mechanic.md#critical-results)
  section, and over-mechanizing them would cut against that.

This was an autonomous pass per the user's instruction (write directly, no per-Gift
confirmation, report at the end). **A monitored review pass is still owed** — the user
asked to go through the results together after this report lands.

  same rest at 2 Resonance per additional Health Level; a recovered Health Level's
  container always fills to full. Marked temporary/not-final, same as the Gift
  activation-cost formula.
- **New Gift added 2026-07-16**: **[Healer's Touch](../rules/premade-gifts.md#healers-touch)**

- **Adaptive Flesh** (Biological & Shapeshifting) — **Level 1 rewritten and confirmed**:
  now a hard guarantee against being caught unaware by an environmental hazard specifically

  moved to its new alphabetical spot between Mimic's Gift and Undying Vigor). User dictated

  Health Level; **L3** replaces that with passive in-container regen at Body÷2/minute (does
  not recover a lost Health Level); **L4** keeps L3's regen, adds 1 Resonance to regrow a
  limb/bone/eye; **L5** the passive rate rises to Body÷2/turn, adds 3 Resonance to recover
  every lost Health Level at once. Cross-references in Bulwark, Healer's Touch, and
  combat.md's new Healing rule all updated to the new name/anchor.

- **Many Shapes** (Biological & Shapeshifting) — fully mechanized. Now has a concrete

  Shape and L4 Any Beast Base +3 (Light), L5 War-Form Base +6 (Martial). Each active shift

  trait mimicry (which overlapped with Many Shapes) to ability mimicry: watch a target use
  a Skill/Perk/Gift, then copy it yourself. Resolution moved from Body to Soul. Guardrails:
  must have watched the target actually use the thing (no copying from description), and
  the mimicked Gift-level cap scales with this Gift's own level (L3 capability at Mimic
  L3, L5 at Mimic L5), not the target's. L4-5 last a full day instead of just a scene. Each
  level now costs Resonance directly: 1/1/2/3/5 across L1-5. Written into the file.

- **Undying Vigor** (Biological & Shapeshifting) — fully mechanized. Advantage/DoS-bonus
  escalation (+1 at L2, +3 at L4) on resisting poison/disease; Expel It (L3) actively
  purges for 1 Resonance, rising to 2 for serious/lethal-tier afflictions at L4; L5 grants
  full passive immunity to natural-origin poison/disease/exhaustion while mystical/
  supernatural afflictions still need an active 3-Resonance purge. **Biological &
  Shapeshifting category is now fully reviewed** except Adaptive Flesh's L2-5 (blocked on
  the hostile-environment mechanic, see above).

- **Body as Weapon** (Combat & Martial Techniques) — L1 got an explicit Unarmed Base +1
  damage rating; L2 was reworked from "no armor penalty" (armor doesn't exist as a system
  in this game, and any toughness framing risks Bulwark's exclusive Soak territory) to a
  flat **+1 Defense**, unconditional. L5's Action column changed from "Free" to "—" since
  the level's whole point is that it costs nothing at all, not even a Free action.
  **Resonance-free by design** — established a pattern here: Gifts granting a discrete,
  on-demand effect (Regeneration, Many Shapes, Mimic's Gift) cost Resonance per use; Gifts
  granting an always-on passive trait or reflexive defense (Bulwark, Untouchable, and now
  Body as Weapon) don't.

- **Marked for the Hunt** (Combat & Martial Techniques) — L1 marking a target now costs
  1 Resonance (was free); L5's pinpoint-location ability lost its once-per-scene cap and
  is gated by a 1-Resonance spend instead, so it can fire more than once a scene if the
  Resonance is there.

- **Opportunist's Edge** (Combat & Martial Techniques) — L1 rewritten from pure flavor to
  a real mechanic (auto-detects an opening within Close range, grants Advantage on the
  next roll against that target); L3 tightened to "an extra Move or Hide, no Action
  spent" instead of the vaguer "move/hide/disengage instead of your Action"; L5 reworded
  for clarity (using Cunning Step also allows a free attack, once per scene). No Resonance
  cost — same always-on/reflexive pattern as Body as Weapon.

- **Reckless Instinct** (Combat & Martial Techniques) — fully reviewed, continuing past
  the prior stopping point. L1: surprise-immunity scoped explicitly to *mundane* ambush
  (Gift/Perk-based ambushes are a separate, later immunity), replaced Advantage with a flat
  **+3 DoS** on the first round's Initiative roll. L2: "All In" no longer costs an Action,
  it's a free declared choice, and its Advantage now applies to *all* attacks that round,
  not just one. L4 reworded to match L2's free-action-cost (was inconsistently still
  costing an Action). L5 redesigned from scratch since L2's change broke its old text
  (used to say triggering Reckless "costs a Free action instead of your whole turn," which
  no longer meant anything once L2 made it free): now escalates the DoS bonus to **+5**
  (completing the 1→3→5 convention) and removes the enemy's retaliatory Advantage
  entirely — at max level, going Reckless stops being a real gamble.

- **Undying Fury** (Combat & Martial Techniques) — surfaced the **self-contained Gifts**
  design decision (now in flavor-writing-style.md): the old L1/L3 text leaned on
  "fatigue-based Disadvantage" and "a sustain roll," neither of which is defined anywhere
  in core-mechanic.md or combat.md. Rewritten so the Gift defines its own limits: **L1**
  now costs 1 Resonance to enter a rage lasting **3 rounds**, and ties its immunity
  directly to the real Health Level penalty table (ignores the -1/-3/-5 across-the-board
  penalty unless Incapacitated) instead of a vague "fatigue" reference; **L2** unchanged
  in substance, now explicitly scoped to the 3-round timer; **L3** removes that timer
  (holds the whole scene, no roll/action/upkeep) instead of removing an undefined "sustain
  roll"; **L4** keeps its once-per-scene cap (a first draft accidentally dropped it — don't
  assume a change removes an existing limiter unless told to) and now costs 1 Resonance,
  changing "ignore the hit" to "the container bottoms out at 1 HP instead of 0"; **L5**
  unchanged. See TODO.md for the still-open sweep of other Gifts using the same undefined
  "fatigue" or bare "Scene (sustained)" language (Second Wind, Undying Vigor, Trick of the
  Light, Last Rites, Resonant Shatter, Borrowed Time, Frozen Instant).

- **Untouchable** (Combat & Martial Techniques) — **rescoped entirely** from a dual
  physical-evasion-and-mental-resistance Gift to a pure mental/Soul defense, per the user's
  direction that a standalone physical-evasion Gift would be "too powerful and too limited
  simultaneously" (base Dodge/Defense already cover physical evasion at the core-mechanic
  level, no dedicated Gift needed). Resolution moved from Mind to Soul, matching
  core-mechanic.md's own definition of Resolve's domain (fear, domination, charm,
  corruption, possession). New unified mechanic: a flat **-1/-2/-3/-4/-5 DoS** penalty on
  any Mind-affecting Gift used against you (one point per Gift Level, not the usual 1/3/5
  step), plus outright immunity to Mind-affecting Gifts up to a level threshold that grows
  **0 (L1) → 1 (L2) → 1 (L3) → 2 (L4) → 3 (L5)** — an attacker can still try past that
  threshold by spending 1 Resonance, but the flat DoS penalty still applies even then.
  Throw It Back (the reflect-an-intrusion ability) survives unchanged at L3. **Combat &
  Martial Techniques is now fully reviewed.**

- **Ember Touch** (Elemental & Energy Manipulation) — reviewed and later folded into
  Elemental Manipulation, see below. Fully restructured with a dual

  penalty on fire-based attacks against you, paired with a harmless-flame size threshold

- **Radiant Burst** (Elemental & Energy Manipulation) — checked for a standalone darkness
