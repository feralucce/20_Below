Instructional Design·Project Management·Visual Information Design

Most of what a producer does on the first morning of a shoot is instructional design: the plan lives in a few heads, and by call time it has to live in everyone’s. I’ve done that for more than 30 years in media production, including 14 years producing independent film and television. Since 2021 I’ve been the producer of 20 Below, a tabletop roleplaying game built by a fifteen-person team, and the case studies here come from that shipped work.

From the Team

How I Work

I’m the hub of the 20 Below team: communications, a language expert, an engineer, a web development consultant, four game designers and seven playtesters. The team brings the problems, the decisions and the final check stay with me, and an AI assistant speeds up the work in between. Every case study credits who did what.

Each study follows the same shape: the problem, who it was for, the options weighed, the decision, and what happened. Where the outcome isn’t measured yet, the study says so.
