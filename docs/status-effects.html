Status Effects

Every status effect currently defined in the system - what triggers it, what it does, and how it clears. This isn't necessarily a permanently closed list; more may be added as the rules keep developing.

From a roll

Off Balance

Trigger: rolling a catastrophic failure on any roll during combat.

Effect: everything you roll is at Disadvantage. It does not stack - you are Off Balance or you are not, however many catastrophic failures you roll in a round.

Clears: on its own at the end of your next turn, with no action or roll needed. A Fate Token spent to Shrug Off an Effect clears it immediately, though that never undoes the separate consequence the catastrophic failure caused. Never Off Balance grants a reroll of the triggering failure itself.

Distracted

Trigger: losing a Health Level, or being the target of a Kotodama effect, while resolving a Slow Action Bracket action. A Gift, an environmental hazard, or GM fiat can also impose it directly, whether or not a Slow action is involved.

Atropos + Difficulty, roll 2d10, to hold focus

Effect: if the trigger happened during a Slow action, failing the roll downgrades that action to a Normal one, losing the called-shot option and the Advantage that made it Slow in the first place. Outside a Slow action, failure instead imposes Disadvantage on whatever triggered it.

Clears: resolved immediately by the roll itself - success or failure, there's nothing lingering afterward. Concentration grants immunity to being Distracted, regardless of the source.

Surprised

Trigger: not noticing a threat before combat begins - typically because an opposing Stealth roll beat your Perception, or the GM judges the fiction calls for it.

Effect: Disadvantage on everything - attacks, and any other roll where being ready matters - for the rest of the round you're caught in.

Clears: automatically, once that round ends - no roll needed. Alertness grants immunity to being Surprised while conscious.

Staggered

Trigger: named by whatever causes it - a blow that lands wrong, a shock, a moment of not knowing which way is up.

Effect: your declared Action Bracket drops one step: Fast becomes Normal, Normal becomes Slow. Declared Slow, you still act, but lose whatever your empowered action would have given.

Clears: 1 Ki per step buys it straight back, the same spend that bumps a band up in the first place. Otherwise it clears at the end of your next turn. Staggered does not stack.

From a Vital at zero

Flustered

Trigger: your Poise reaching 0.

Effect: Disadvantage on all Social rolls, and on anything else where composure matters (GM's call), for the rest of the Scene.

Presence + Difficulty, roll 2d10, to shake it off early

Clears: lasts the rest of the Scene by default, but you can spend an action to roll Presence against a Difficulty and end it immediately on success. No Gift currently grants immunity to Flustered - intentional.

Humiliated

Trigger: your Poise dropping below 0.

Effect: you cannot take the lead, negotiate, or be trusted to speak for the group - socially deferring and complying rather than asserting yourself. Unlike Shattered, you still act fully on your own; this is social paralysis, not physical.

Clears: once Poise is restored to 0. While below 0, a rest or 1 Ki brings Poise back to 1 only, never to full.

Overwhelmed

Trigger: your Sanity reaching 0.

Effect: a temporary negative mental trait, plus Disadvantage on your rolls. You can still act on your own.

Clears: once you're removed from whatever caused it and get a chance to rest, or by spending 1 Ki - which also refills Sanity back to full.

Shattered

Trigger: your Sanity dropping below 0.

Effect: panicky, babbling, unable to act on your own - someone has to lead or drag you.

Clears: the same two ways as Overwhelmed - removal-plus-rest, or 1 Ki - but either one only restores Sanity to 1, not fully; normal recovery resumes from there. Dropping below 0 always leaves a permanent mental scar, even if you recover with Ki.

Unconscious, then Dying

Trigger: your Health Levels reaching 0, and then dropping below it.

Effect: at 0 you fall unconscious and cannot act. Below 0 you are Dying: still unconscious, still losing ground. The negative range mirrors the positive one, so reaching minus your full Health Levels is death.

Clears: below 0, a Short Rest recovers nothing and a Full Night's Rest buys back 1 Health Level, and only one, until you are back to 0. Unstoppable lets a character stay conscious and act throughout instead of blacking out at 0.

From the world

Exhausted

Trigger: pushing a sustained physical exertion (forced marching, hard labor, running flat-out) past its free baseline duration, set by your Stamina. Each additional unit of time beyond that baseline requires a roll. Cold and heat impose levels too, on a timed exposure rate rather than a roll.

Water + Difficulty, roll 2d10 (Difficulty drops each successive check)

Effect: cumulative, stacking one level at a time on a failed check:

LevelEffect
1Disadvantage on Physical rolls
2Disadvantage on all rolls
3Movement Rate halved; no Fast actions
4Every Ki spend costs +1
5Unconscious, until warmed, cooled, or rested

Clears: a Short Rest drops one level; a Full Night's Rest clears it entirely - the same Short/Full split Health Level recovery uses. A level taken from an environment cannot be cleared while you are still in it: resting in the cold does not clear cold.

Blinded

Trigger: darkness you have no answer for, a flash, a faceful of something.

Effect: Disadvantage on anything that needs sight. You neither use nor suffer any effect that requires seeing or being seen - no eye contact, no reading the room, no Gift that depends on the target looking at you.

Clears: with whatever caused it. Heightened Senses can sidestep it entirely.

Deafened

Trigger: an explosion, a pressure wave, water, a Gift.

Effect: Disadvantage on anything that needs hearing. You neither use nor suffer any effect that has to be heard to work.

Clears: with whatever caused it. A Deafened character can still speak, so a Kotodama of their own is unaffected.

Grabbed

Trigger: being held - a hand, a jaw, a coil, a will.

Effect: your Movement Rate is 0, and you roll at Disadvantage on everything except escaping or attacking whatever holds you.

Clears: a contested roll against the grabber's Potence - or against its Soak where the grip is a thing rather than a person, GM's call - or when the grabber lets go.

Prone

Trigger: knocked flat - a blow, a fall, or footing that gave out. Prone is always inflicted; going down on purpose is Belly Down.

Effect: you can't attack, every attack against you gains Advantage, and you can't Crawl.

Clears: one action spent recovering, which is a Fast character's entire turn.

Frightened

Trigger: something too much to face. Frightened always has a source, and the condition is of that source rather than of the world in general.

Effect: Disadvantage on rolls against the source, and you cannot willingly move closer to it. You can still fight it if it comes to you, still run, still act normally on everything else.

Clears: when the source can no longer be perceived, or at the end of the Scene, whichever comes first.

Envenomed

Trigger: venom, toxin, or a drug in the blood. Venomous creatures impose it on a successful hit, and each bestiary entry says so.

Effect: Disadvantage on Physical rolls. It does not clear on a rest - the body cannot sleep this one off.

Clears: once a day, starting the day after, roll Water + 8, and one success ends it. The roll is never at Disadvantage; a successful Medicine roll that day gives it Advantage, but Medicine never ends it on its own. Antitoxin always ends it. Ki cannot. A creature whose venom works differently says so in its own entry.

Bleeding

Trigger: named by whatever opened the wound.

Effect: 1 die against Soak at the end of each round, resolved per-die like any other attack, a connecting die costing a Health Level as usual.

Clears: a Normal action to stem it, spent by you or by somebody who reaches you, or any Gift that closes wounds. Being on fire is the same shape at 2 dice per round.
