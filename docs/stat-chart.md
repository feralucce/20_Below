# Stat Chart (Reference Snapshot)

A quick-reference summary of the current Attribute/sub-stat state. **This is a snapshot, not the source of truth** — if it ever conflicts with [rules/rules.md](../rules/rules.md) or [rules/fate.md](../rules/fate.md), those win. Update this file whenever the underlying mechanics change.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Earth** | Physical power/endurance | **Soak** | Passive wall — each incoming damage die compared individually; die ≤ Soak is fully absorbed, die > Soak connects. Soak 10 = guaranteed 0% connect (true full negation, unless the attacker spends Ki to boost individual dice via Ki Infusion). | **Potence** | **Confirmed 2026-08-09, fully scaled 2026-08-12** — two jobs: (1) flat passive give, Carrying Capacity (`Potence² × 10` kg) + Break Threshold (Potence ≥ target's Soak = automatic no-roll break), no roll; (2) contested dice pool reusing the per-die comparison shape — Potence itself (not a weapon) sets d10 count, each die vs. the target's Soak (creatures and objects alike, plus a reference chart of Soak/Health Levels for common objects). |

Each Level is a binary hit-box — connects once, then it's gone. At 0, unconscious; at Health Levels &le; &minus;(Health sub-stat), dead. |

(2) Lucky Number — any die rolled for the character landing on a result equal to their Klotho rating automatically grants 1 Fate Token. |

## Separate Pools/Resources (not sub-stats)

- **Ki** — average of all five combat sub-stats (Soak, Initiative, Ferocity, Stamina, Atropos), doubled. Target number for the Fatigue Check; depletes on failure, refilled by rest (Short Rest restores Klotho/2, Full Night's Rest restores fully); can also be spent directly — 1 Ki preserves 1 Health Level, 1 Ki per die for [Ki Infusion](../rules/rules.md#ki-infusion) on **any** attack type (Physical/Social/Mental, unified 2026-08-10), or 1 Ki per step to [Bump Action Bracket](../rules/rules.md#action-brackets).
- **Fate Tokens** — the earn/spend currency. Gained via Nature, voluntary Disadvantage, Flaws, daily regen (flat 1 at sunrise, resolved 2026-08-12), Milestones, GM discretion. Spent on Kotodama (formerly separate Push the Fiction/Narrative Control, merged and renamed 2026-08-10), plus Overcome a Flaw, Automatic Success, Boost a Check, and Shrug Off an Effect, all flat 1 token, resolved 2026-08-12. **Bump Action Bracket and Fate Token Infusion both moved to direct Ki spends, 2026-08-10.**
