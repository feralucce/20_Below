Changelog

Newest entries first. Starts 2026-08-20 - not backdated to earlier development history.

Rules

  • 10-01-2026 The rules pass for the playtest PDF. Dying no longer slides: only more damage moves you toward death, you're dead at minus your Health Levels, a Fate Token gets you back up at 1 even while you're out cold, and stabilizing brings you to 0. Fate Tokens: hold (Stamina x 3) + 1, and make Stamina + 1 counted spends a Scene - a Kotodama, or each level of Corruption you burn off. The Lucky Number is the seventh way to earn a Token. Scars replace Flaws from going below 0: Physical, Social and Mental, sixty of them, healed in Days by a Gift, Weeks by an Adder or Months by a Skill, with two new Gifts, Restoration and Sanctuary, and rules for making your own. Corruption is one source with many loci, five levels from Tell to Claimed. Vehicles hit for 1 die per 10 MPH (16 km/h), with no cap. Threadspace gets a proper explanation. Opposed rolls, helping, trying again, group rolls and taking your time are real rules now. NPCs are built like player characters, and an NPC with no Gift has 1 Ki. Flaws are Leveled, 1 to 5. And from a final sync check: 1 Ki at or below 0 brings Poise or Sanity back to 1, and Resource Index 6 can't be tried below Level 4.
  • 9-28-2026 Movement gets a full chapter that works two ways, theater of the mind in meters or a map at one hex to the meter, and Melee reaches about a meter. Armor covers four Zones (Center of Mass, Head, Arms and Legs), power armor is out and ten new pieces are in, and things now have Hardness and Health Levels from a materials table. Corruption arrives as a five-level track for things that change you slowly. The bestiary gets attacks by role, boosted natural weapons, dice on every line, sub-stats and Mental Defense on every creature, and one venom rule. Time Bands run out to Century. Gifts gain 277 Adders and 192 Limiters, a second form or loadout gets its own full pool, and Conjured Armory, Forcefield and Peripheral are clarified. Perception is always an Everyman Skill, and the Novice Skill tier is retired, with Reset Skills in its place.
  • 9-21-2026 Every Gift description is rewritten, Claws / Fangs is retired, and the Cybernetics build menu widens to 57 options. Boons: Ambidextrous becomes three tiers and the off-hand penalty is finally stated, Features becomes Distinctive Features with real effects, Never Winded folds into Weathered, Speed Reading into Quick Study, and Oathbound becomes Oathbinder, a Gift. Anomalous Objects arrive: made things, granted by the GM, never bought. Equipment and creation-Wealth are rebuilt on a geometric scale, with Starting Packages, an Occult & Warding section and Beyond the Ordinary tables. The Natures move into Creating a Character, rewritten, with triggers that say how they pay. Eight new status effects: Staggered, Blinded, Deafened, Grabbed, Prone, Frightened, Envenomed and Bleeding, and Surprise is now Surprised.
  • 9-14-2026 The Vitals get their names, Dying gets its, and nobody dies five Levels early any more. Poise has a floor, and hitting it costs a Sanity Level. Damage runs on a halved scale, Gift dice grow with the character, and a critical decides dice instead of doubling them. Social attacks run on Training rather than a GM rating. XP can buy Ki. Skills are rewritten from the book, cut to 77 with their Elements rebalanced, and a weapon Skill now says what you can pick up rather than how well you swing. Special Weapons becomes a Boon, and Necromancy gets four Adders.
  • 9-07-2026 Ki is reworked into a common pool, your strongest Element plus 8, with no Fatigue Check and a Gift Check against what's left. The Fate Token holding cap moves to Stamina. A catastrophic failure in combat leaves you Off Balance, and Ki Infusion rolls the dice first and lets you choose what to boost. There are now three Defenses, one per attack type, and Defense rises with size. Mental attacks need a Gift, Social attacks get their own damage dice, Signature Move picks its power and its wall separately, and the GM's pool gets a mechanic of its own, with the tides of Fate. Below zero mirrors above zero. The Range Bands get real distances, Sprint arrives, and so do Common Hazards and a definition for Exhausted. Overwhelmed and Shattered join the Conditions list, Battle Sense and Duelist give attacks the ladder no Training Tier reaches, and sixty more gear packages arrive.
  • 8-31-2026 Glossary expanded and rewritten in the Player’s Guide’s voice - 68 entries to 76, adding Attack, Wall, Called Shot, Condition, and Level, plus separate entries for Fast, Normal, and Slow Actions. “Wall” in particular was jargon leaned on across four entries and never actually defined anywhere. Called shots broadened from head-only to anything specific you can name - a hand, a knee, the strap holding a pack on, a camera housing, the weapon pointed at you - with what landing one accomplishes left to the GM; the head stays the one case the armor Zone system models. Spending Ki to bump the Action Bracket now carries a called shot along with it, while Distracted’s forced downgrade still strips it.
  • 8-24-2026 Short Rest recovery now always heals at least 1 (Health Levels, Poise, Sanity, Ki) - the round-up-half-your-sub-stat rate could heal 0 outright at sub-stat 0, since half of 0 rounds up to 0.
  • 8-23-2026 Skills now default to an Attribute/Element (new Default Element column on Premade Skills) instead of pairing freely - a player can still challenge the default with a different Attribute, but it has to be grounded in a Descriptor, same as before. Descriptors are now firmly core traits: the old "buy an extra Descriptor" option (Discretionary points at creation, XP after) is gone entirely - a broader spread comes only from raising the sub-stat. Sample Descriptors across every sub-stat rewritten from evocative phrases to tight one/two-word adjectives (Brawny, Brutal, Indefatigable, Headstrong, and so on), Laws of the Night-style. Moira's share of the Skill list widened on direct follow-up ("we need a lot more for Moira"): Divination, Precognition, and Ritual Craft added as three new Skills, and Hunting, Insight, Investigation, Navigation, Religion, Streetwise, and Tracking reassigned from their original default to Moira wherever an instinct/fate/luck reading genuinely fit the Skill - 12 Skills now default Moira, up from 2.
  • 8-22-2026 1200 Added a Gifts Pool leftover rule: Limiters vary a Gift's per-Level cost, so the 21-point pool doesn't always divide evenly - unspent points now convert into Discretionary points at 2:1, same idea as the Boons Pool's existing 1:1 conversion.
  • 8-21-2026 2100 Added Everyman Gear Packages: 18 free, no-roll starter kits (3 per creation-Wealth Level, 0-5) as an alternative to Wealth Check shopping, access cumulative downward. New Flaw, Destitute, zeroes creation-Wealth and unlocks the exclusive Level 0 tier.
  • 8-21-2026 1200 Clarified Jack of all Trades: unspent Skills Pool points under Tier 1's Trained cap don't convert into points for anything else.
  • 8-20-2026 1445 Every entry in the Adversary Index (Mundane Beasts + Beast Variants), Cryptids, and Nightmare Creatures now has a real 3-5 sentence description covering appearance, behavior, and what makes it distinct at the table, instead of a single one-line flavor hook.
  • 8-20-2026 1340 Reformatted Resources: each of the 23 Resources now gets its own Level table instead of being crammed 2-4 to a table, plus a jump list to navigate straight to one. No mechanical changes - table layout only.
  • 8-20-2026 1251 New Creation-Wealth Reference doc, spelling out the gear-shopping procedure at character creation step by step, with a flowchart diagram. Closes the second of two "new reference docs" flagged since RP-10 - Advancement Reference was the first.
  • 8-20-2026 1209 Balance-tested Bow dice ratings against the Soak math, split into three tiers: Recurve bow raised to 5 (Handgun-tier), new Compound bow entry at 7 (Rifle-tier), Crossbow raised to 7 to match, new English longbow entry at 10 (Heavy-tier). Closes the last of weapons.md's four tracked sub-gaps.
  • 8-20-2026 1147 Added an Ammo Recovery rule for Bow and Thrown weapons: recover half the ammo spent (rounded down) if you control the battlefield after a fight, unlike firearm cartridges which aren't reusable.
  • 8-20-2026 1130 Added reload rules, a longstanding gap: a new Reload Combat Action, with cost varying by how the weapon actually loads (magazine/speed-loader/chain-fed and crossbow take one action, a tube-fed shotgun takes a Slow action, a quiver-drawn bow reloads free). New Reload column on weapons.md's Basic Weapons table.
  • 8-20-2026 1020 Built out the Scars mechanic fully: Poise now goes negative (new Humiliated status, no death threshold), Mental Scars finally defined (was flagged as TBD), Battle Scars now accumulate and can be healed away after a Month by Healing/Regeneration. New Flaw: Shaken Confidence. New docs/scars.html with 27 Physical, 23 Mental, and 15 Social scar flavor options.
  • 8-20-2026 0927 Added 9 new Boons (Animal Rapport, Compact Frame, Iron Grip, Lie Detector, Never Winded, Quick Study, Restful Trance, Unshakeable Alibi, Weathered). Also newly defined Short Rest/Full Night's Rest duration and frequency, a real rules gap Restful Trance surfaced.
  • 8-20-2026 0841 Added 3 new Flaws: Item of Power (a Gift depends on an external item), Ritual Required (a Gift needs preparation time), Incantations (a Gift needs a spoken trigger).
  • 8-20-2026 0830 Added 14 new Resources (Safehouse Network, Underworld Reputation, Medical Access, Legal Counsel, Media Contact, Black Market Access, Spiritual Standing, Corporate Backing, Informant Network, Signature Possession, Sanctuary Standing, Guild Standing, Debts Owed, Territory), full 5-level tables for each.
  • 8-20-2026 0817 Resource creation cost halved: 1 point per level instead of 2 (pool stays 6 points, now buys 6 levels of Resources total instead of 3).
  • 8-20-2026 0812 Created changelog. Added 6 Gifts (Necromancy, Drone Swarm, Berserker, Conjured Armory, Memory Manipulation, Forcefield) and 1 Boon (Always Connected). Defined Milestone Award, the Combat Actions list, and Limiter buy-off costs. Various fixes and cleanup.

App

  • 10-01-2026 Character Creator v0.12.35 to v0.12.39, Battle Tracker v0.3.5 to v0.3.9, The Brewery v0.4.11 to v0.4.13, Encounter Difficulty Calculator v0.2.5 to v0.2.8. Report a bug: the Character Creator and the Battle Tracker have a Report a bug button that sends a numbered ticket straight to us, filed under the step or part of the app where it happened. If a future rules change needs a newer version, you get an update screen with a download button instead of an app running on rules it can’t read; offline, they open as normal. On the sheet, the minus button spends a Token freely, and the box beside it, now Counted This Scene, is clicked for each Kotodama or level of Corruption you burn, stopping at Stamina + 1. Scars are split into Physical, Social and Mental, on the Scars tab and on page 5 of the sheet. Skill descriptions open from a Skill’s name on the Skills page, the way they already did on Discretionary Points. The Encounter Difficulty Calculator weighs fights properly again: creatures printed since the bestiary revision were being read as rolling 1 die, which made nearly every fight look Trivial. From the 10-01 sync check (Creator v0.12.39, Battle Tracker v0.3.9, Brewery v0.4.13, Calculator v0.2.8): Social scars show as Social in the Battle Tracker and the Owlbear Character Sheet, where they had shown as Mental or not at all; an NPC with no Gift has 1 Ki; Resource Index 6 can't be tried below Level 4; and the Brewery cuts a table taller than a page between its rows, repeating the header.

Novice is gone: Skills run Untrained, Trained, Adept, Expert, Master, and saved characters convert on opening, with any points that only paid for Novice handed back. Moving between apps: characters pass between the Creator and the new Owlbear Rodeo Character Sheet through your Downloads folder on the same computer. Battle Tracker v0.3.0 is a bestiary and rogue’s gallery with combat built in: every creature from the free bestiary packs, your own characters and NPCs with tags and search, saved encounters you can carry into Owlbear Rodeo, and Public or Secret rolls on every line. Boons, Flaws and Resources show their descriptions on their cards, as Gifts always have. Gifts and Boons reworked: Claws / Fangs is folded into Onslaught, and 44 Boons become 41. The Brewery gains a ::: item block for laying gear out as a card, with its Wealth rating lifted into a pill.
  • 9-14-2026 Character Creator v0.10.11 to v0.10.15, Battle Tracker v0.2.5 to v0.2.7, The Brewery v0.3.8 to v0.4.2, Encounter Difficulty Calculator v0.2.2 to v0.2.4. Characters were dying five Levels early. Below 0 now reads Dying, and the Poise and Sanity floors fire in both apps instead of existing only in the rules text. Critical hits follow the book in the Creator: half your dice, rounded up, connect automatically, and the rest roll with your Klotho added. XP can buy Ki, one point at a time, and the Advancement tab, which had been a blank panel, works again. The Brewery had a big week: page backgrounds, page numbers including roman front matter, a book split into one file per chapter laying out on the correct side of the spine, Remove breaks beside Add page breaks, columns that fill the page instead of balancing, long entries continuing overleaf instead of being clipped, and an Examples menu. From v0.4.0, Print / PDF on the desktop app writes a real PDF with selectable text and embedded fonts, at KDP bleed size.
  • 9-07-2026 Character Creator v0.10.4 to v0.10.10, The Brewery v0.3.2 to v0.3.7, Battle Tracker v0.2.2 to v0.2.4, and a new app, the Encounter Difficulty Calculator, v0.1.0 to v0.2.1. Three Defenses: Physical is 10 − Atropos, Social is 10 − Psyche and Mental is 10 − Presence, and the sheet shows all three. Atropos now only covers physical attacks. Signature Move is built, not picked: choose the sub-stat that powers it and the wall it hits separately, and buy more than one Move, each with its own name and Levels. Rules the app carries: Range Bands with real distances, a double move, Sprint, social attacks, Exhausted, and Boost a Check removed as a Fate spend. Get the update: when a newer release exists, a button under the version light opens its installer, and the light stops claiming you need an update when you are already on something newer. On your phone: the web Creator at 20belowrpg.com/app/ can be added to your home screen, works without a signal after the first visit, and a badge says whether it is Offline ready. Encounter Difficulty Calculator: load the party, add the creatures you are considering, and it tells you whether the fight is trivial, standard, hard, deadly or overwhelming, with the count of each creature that reaches each band. The Battle Tracker builds this week have no changes you would notice.
  • 9-05-2026 1230 Desktop v0.10.3, catching the app up to a day of rules work. Off Balance — the condition a catastrophic failure in combat leaves you in, Disadvantage on everything until the end of your next turn, non-stacking, cleared early by spending a Fate Token. v0.10.2 shipped a few hours earlier and had none of it, so looking it up in the app found nothing while the website already had it. The GM’s Fate pool has a mechanic: it buys a Twist, a Claim priced like a player’s own Kotodama, or Hardening that raises the cost of the next claim — and it never undoes a claim that was already paid for. Telepathy’s forced read now rolls 4d10 at Level 3 and 5d10 at Level 4, matching every other Mental and Social attack in the game, all of which size their dice by the Gift Level they’re used at.
  • 9-04-2026 2015 The other two desktop apps catch up with their own source. The Brewery v0.3.1: an aside now tints its title as well as its left rule, the way a box always has — the Syntax sample had been saying “colour tints the rule and the title” while the shipped build did only half of it. Its Element colours are also no longer marked “proposed”; they are the style guide’s Elements palette, same hex values, so a supplement laid out in the Brewery matches the core book. Battle Tracker v0.2.1: the September 1 build was missing real work — dropping a scene token and linking it to its character in one step (the reason the Bestiary extension was folded in rather than left beside it), token size taken from the image’s own dimensions so a wide creature lands wide, critical bands widening from an Element raised past its roll cap, and “critical failure” renamed to catastrophic failure to match the book.
  • 9-04-2026 1947 Desktop v0.10.2, creation-Wealth fixed and then settled. Gear shopping can end. Creation-Wealth kept “dropping to 1” and stopping there, so a Wealth 1 or 2 item stayed affordable however many checks had already failed — one character walked off with fourteen baseball bats. The floor belongs on the cost of a failed check, which the roller already applied; it was being applied to the result as well. Creation-Wealth reaches 0 now and the shopping pass ends as written, and the page says so instead of every Buy button quietly going grey. Destitute wins at creation: a Destitute character shops at creation-Wealth 0, takes a Level 0 Everyman package, and cannot spend points on Wealth until the Flaw is bought off — blocked in both directions, with a line on the card saying why. XP raises Wealth normally once play begins. Two rules files had been carrying contradictory answers on this, which is how the app ended up with two different behaviours in two weeks; Resources, Flaws and Weapons & Equipment now all say the same thing.
  • 9-04-2026 1913 Desktop v0.10.1, three bugs from live play. Cards stay open while you spend — clicking a + on a twirled-out card used to shut it, so raising a Gift meant reopening it every time; open cards were matched by their own label across a redraw, and the label is exactly what changes when you buy a level. The twirls on Boons, Skills, Gifts, Resources and Flaws were never preserved at all, and are now. Buying Wealth works again: visiting the Flaws step made every character read as Destitute whether or not they had taken it, which dropped creation-Wealth to 0, showed only Level 0 Everyman gear packages however much Wealth had been bought, and cut the shop from 227 items to 177. Taking Destitute and then spending points on Wealth now climbs you back out of it. A Flaw could strand the app — Berserk’s text links to Action Brackets, and clicking it landed on an “asset not found” screen with no way back, since the desktop app has no browser chrome to go back with; all 84 cross-references render as plain text now, still marked as terms defined elsewhere. And Discretionary Points tells you what things are: every row twirls out its own definition, including the full five-level Resource table, which previously showed one line with no way to learn what Level 3 actually bought you.
  • 9-03-2026 1020 The Brewer is now The Brewery, and it has themes. A theme sets a page’s colours and its typefaces together, because type carries most of what makes a page feel like its genre — pick one from the Theme menu. Two to start: 20 Below Core, the look the tool has always had, and Backwater Static, set in Fraunces and Source Serif 4 on swamp-black. Every setting and genre will get its own, rolled in as they are released. Greyscale is now a print mode applied to a theme rather than a replacement for one, so a black-and-white interior keeps its own setting’s grey ramp instead of a generic one. There is a desktop version now too — it installs separately from the Character Creator, with all six typefaces built in, so it lays out and prints the same with no internet at all.
  • 9-03-2026 0808 Desktop v0.10.0, catching the app up to the current rules. The app also renders offline now: the markdown renderer used to load from a CDN, which meant an installed app with no network could not display its own rules text.
